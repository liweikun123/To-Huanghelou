# China Cannot Recapitalise Demand
# 中国无法为“需求”注资

**Desmond Shum**  
**沈栋**

**Sep 07, 2026**  
**2026年9月7日**

---

China has just launched another major recapitalisation of its state financial system.  
中国刚刚对其国有金融体系启动了新一轮大规模的注资重组（Capital Injection/Recapitalisation）。

Eight central-government financial institutions will raise around RMB 360 billion in new capital.  
八家中央级金融机构将筹集约 3,600 亿元人民币的新增资本。

The Ministry of Finance will provide RMB 300 billion through special treasury bonds, with the rest coming from other state entities.  
财政部将通过特别国债提供 3,000 亿元，其余部分来自于其他国有实体。

The recipients include ICBC and Agricultural Bank, as well as China Life, PICC, China Taiping, China Re, Export-Import Bank and China Export & Credit Insurance Corporation.  
注资对象包括工商银行和农业银行，以及中国人寿、中国人保、中国太平、中国再保险、中国进出口银行和中国出口信用保险公司。

This follows the RMB 520 billion recapitalisation of four major state banks in 2025.  
在此之前，中国已于 2025 年完成了对四家大型国有银行 5,200 亿元人民币的注资。

Put together, Beijing has injected or arranged around RMB 880 billion, or roughly US$130 billion, of new capital into major financial institutions in less than eighteen months.  
加在一起，北京在不到 18 个月的时间里，已向主要金融机构注入或安排了约 8,800 亿元人民币（约合 1,300 亿美元）的新增资本。

More importantly, all six of China’s major state-owned commercial banks have now been recapitalised, and the programme has expanded beyond banks into insurers and policy financial institutions.  
更重要的是，中国全部六大国有商业银行目前均已完成注资重组，且该计划已从银行扩展至保险公司和政策性金融机构。

That is what makes this important.  
这正是此事非同寻常之处。

---

## Why do supposedly healthy institutions need this much capital?
## 为什么表面上健康的机构需要这么多资本？

The official position is that these institutions remain healthy, with stable asset quality and adequate regulatory ratios.  
官方的立场是：这些机构依然健康，资产质量稳定，监管指标充足。

But if that is really the case, why do they need hundreds of billions of renminbi in fresh state capital?  
但如果事实果真如此，为什么它们还需要数千亿元人民币的新增国家资本？

I think there are two answers.  
我认为有两个答案。

The first is straightforward financial deterioration.  
第一个答案是直接的财务状况恶化。

Loans connected to local governments, property developers and households have all come under pressure as the underlying economy weakens.  
随着底层经济放缓，与地方政府、房地产开发商和居民家庭相关的贷款全部面临压力。

Commercial-bank net interest margins had fallen to around 1.41 per cent by June 2026, while the large state commercial banks averaged only around 1.31 per cent.  
到 2026 年 6 月，商业银行的净息差已降至 1.41% 左右，而大型国有商业银行的平均净息差仅为 1.31% 左右。

For context, 1.8 per cent has traditionally been used as an important benchmark in China’s banking assessment framework for a healthy net interest margin.  
作为背景，在传统上，1.8% 一向被视为中国银行评估体系中衡量净息差健康与否的重要基准线。

Below that level, a bank receives progressively lower scores on this measure.  
低于这一水平，银行在该指标上的得分就会递减。

So 1.41 per cent is not just “lower than before”; it represents a banking system operating with margins already well below the traditional benchmark needed to generate profits, replenish capital and absorb future losses.  
因此，1.41% 不仅仅是“比以前低”；它代表着整个银行系统的利润率已经远低于产生利润、补充资本和吸收未来亏损所需的传统基准水平。

Even if officially recognised non-performing loans remain manageable, weaker asset quality, lower returns and thinner margins gradually erode financial institutions’ capacity to absorb losses and replenish their own balance sheets.  
即使官方认定的不良贷款率依然处于可控水平，资产质量下降、回报率降低和息差收窄也会逐渐侵蚀金融机构吸收损失和自我修复资产负债表的能力。

Eventually, someone has to provide new capital.  
最终，必须有人来提供新的资本。

The second reason is equally important: Beijing is asking these institutions to perform increasingly large state functions.  
第二个原因同样重要：北京正在要求这些机构承担日益繁重的国家职能。

Banks are expected to support local governments, refinance weak borrowers, lower lending costs, channel money into strategic industries, maintain financial stability and expand credit to support economic growth.  
银行被寄予厚望，要支持地方政府、为脆弱的借款人提供再融资、降低融资成本、向战略性产业输送资金、维护金融稳定以及扩大信贷以支撑经济增长。

Insurers, meanwhile, are being encouraged to provide “long-term capital” to the stock market and broader financial system even as low interest rates weaken the returns on their own investment portfolios.  
与此同时，保险公司正在被鼓励为股市和更广泛的金融体系提供“长期资本”，哪怕低利率环境正在削弱它们自身投资组合的回报率。

So these institutions are being squeezed from both directions.  
因此，这些机构正在遭受双向挤压。

Their underlying economics are deteriorating while the state is asking them to do more.  
它们的底层经济效益在恶化，而国家却要求它们承担更多职责。

This does not necessarily mean they are insolvent.  
这并不一定意味着它们破产倒闭了。

It means their ability to carry out the functions demanded of them is increasingly exceeding their ability to generate the capital required to do so.  
这意味着它们履行被要求的职能的能力，正日益超出其自我造血以产生所需资本的能力。

---

## This is becoming a system-wide problem
## 这正在演变成一个系统性问题

China has recapitalised its banks before.  
中国过去也曾对银行进行过注资重组。

The current 2025–26 round, at around RMB 880 billion, is already equivalent to roughly 0.6 per cent of 2025 GDP.  
目前 2025 至 2026 年这轮注资规模约为 8,800 亿元人民币，已相当于 2025 年 GDP 的 0.6% 左右。

This is no longer about one troubled institution or one isolated balance sheet.  
这不再关乎单家陷入困境的机构或某张孤立的资产负债表。

The recapitalisation now encompasses commercial banks, insurers and policy financial institutions.  
现在的注资重组已经涵盖了商业银行、保险公司和政策性金融机构。

At the same time, local governments are undergoing their own enormous balance-sheet restructuring.  
与此同时，地方政府也在经历自身巨额的资产负债表重组。

Beijing’s latest 债换债 — debt-swap programme allows trillions of renminbi of hidden local-government debt to be converted into formal government borrowing at lower interest rates and longer maturities.  
北京最新的“债换债”计划，允许数万亿元人民币的高风险地方政府隐性债务转换为利率更低、期限更长的正式政府借款。

The programme authorised an additional RMB 6 trillion of local-government bond issuance over three years to replace hidden debt, plus another RMB 4 trillion of existing special-bond capacity over five years for the same purpose.  
该计划授权在三年内额外发行 6 万亿元人民币的地方政府债券来置换隐性债务，并在五年内安排另外 4 万亿元现有的专项债限额用于同一目的。

Technically, much of that remains local-government debt.  
从技术层面上看，其中大部分依然属于地方政府债务。

Economically, however, the direction is clear: weak, opaque liabilities are being moved into stronger and more formal parts of the state financial system, increasingly backed by Beijing.  
然而在经济实质上，趋势十分明确：脆弱、不透明的负债正在被转移至国家金融体系中更强壮、更正式的部分，并且越来越依靠北京的信用背书。

The same process is visible everywhere.  
同样的景象随处可见。

Weak financial institutions are merged. Local-government liabilities are refinanced. Major banks are recapitalised. Insurers are recapitalised. Policy institutions are recapitalised.  
脆弱的金融机构被合并，地方政府的债务被再融资，大型银行被注资重组，保险公司被注资重组，政策性机构被注资重组。

One part of the system after another is becoming less capable of carrying out its functions without support from above.  
系统中的一个又一个板块，在没有上级支持的情况下，履行其职能的能力都在下降。

Eventually, you arrive at the last balance sheet standing: the central government.  
最终，你来到了最后一张依然挺立的资产负债表前：中央政府。

China’s central government still has considerable financial capacity.  
中国中央政府依然拥有相当雄厚的财政与金融实力。

It controls the sovereign balance sheet, the banking system and, ultimately, the monetary machinery.  
它控制着主权资产负债表、银行体系以及最终的货币印钞机。

That is why no one should expect a sudden financial collapse.  
这就是为什么没有人应该预期会出现突然的金融崩溃。

Beijing can extend maturities, convert debt, merge institutions and inject capital for a long time.  
北京可以在相当长的时间内延长期限、转换债务、合并机构并注入资本。

But that does not mean the underlying problems are being solved.  
但这并不意味着底层问题正在得到解决。

It means they are increasingly being absorbed by the centre.  
这只意味着这些问题正在越来越多地由中央吸收。

---

## The recapitalisation helps the financial system. It does much less for the economy.
## 注资重组有助于金融体系，但对实体经济收效甚微

This is the part I think matters more.  
这恰恰是我认为更为关键的部分。

China’s main economic problem is not a shortage of capital.  
中国核心的经济问题绝非资金短缺。

It is a shortage of demand.  
而是需求的短缺。

In 2025, when Beijing recapitalised the major banks, the Ministry of Finance argued that the additional capital could support roughly RMB 6 trillion of extra lending capacity.  
在 2025 年北京对大型银行进行注资重组时，财政部曾论证称，新增资本可以支撑大约 6 万亿元人民币的额外信贷供给能力。

That is an enormous increase in the potential supply of credit.  
这是信贷潜在供应量的一次巨额增加。

Yet by July 2026, new yuan loans actually contracted by RMB 340 billion in a single month.  
然而到 2026 年 7 月，新的人民币贷款在单月中实际上缩减了 3,400 亿元。

Household loans fell by RMB 460.3 billion, corporate loans fell by RMB 130 billion, and outstanding yuan loan growth dropped to around 5.1 per cent, the lowest level on record.  
住户贷款减少了 4,603 亿元，企事业单位贷款减少了 1,300 亿元，人民币各项贷款余额增速降至 5.1% 左右，创下有记录以来的历史新低。

Private investment tells the same story.  
民间投资也反映出同样的困境。

During the first seven months of 2026, total fixed-asset investment fell 6.7 per cent.  
在 2026 年的前七个月里，全国固定资产总投资下降了 6.7%。

Private investment fell 9.4 per cent, and even excluding real estate, private investment was down 5.7 per cent.  
民间投资下降了 9.4%，即便是扣除房地产因素，民间投资依然下降了 5.7%。

These numbers tell us where the real constraint lies.  
这些数据告诉了我们真正的瓶颈究竟何在。

Households facing falling property wealth and economic uncertainty are reluctant to increase leverage.  
面临房产财富缩水和经济不确定性的家庭，不愿意再增加杠杆。

Private businesses facing weak demand, uncertain returns and political uncertainty see fewer attractive reasons to invest.  
面临需求疲软、回报前景不明以及政治不确定性的民营企业，找不到吸引人的理由去扩大投资。

A bank can be given more capital, but it cannot force a household to take out a mortgage.  
银行可以获得更多的资本，但它无法强迫一个家庭去申请房贷。

It cannot make an entrepreneur believe that a new factory will earn an adequate return.  
它无法让一位企业家相信新建一家工厂能赚取合理的回报。

It cannot restore confidence simply by expanding its balance sheet.  
它不可能仅仅通过扩张自己的资产负债表来重塑信心。

That is why I think this recapitalisation will improve the financial health of these institutions and allow them to continue fulfilling state directives, but will do relatively little by itself for the real economy.  
这就是为什么我认为，本次注资重组固然会改善这些机构的财务健康状况，使其能够继续执行国家指令，但其本身对实体经济的推动作用微乎其微。

The constraint is not the supply of lending. It is the demand for it.  
经济的制约因素从来不是贷款的供应，而是对贷款的需求。

For much of the reform era, China’s economic problems were problems of scarcity: not enough roads, factories, housing, electricity or capital.  
在改革开放的大部分时期，中国的经济问题是“匮乏”的问题：公路不足、工厂不足、住房不足、电力不足或资金不足。

The answer was therefore to build more, lend more and invest more.  
因此，当时的解药就是多建、多贷、多投资。

Today China has enormous productive capacity, enormous savings and some of the largest banks in the world.  
如今的中国拥有庞大的生产能力、巨额的储蓄和世界上体量最大的几家银行。

What it lacks is sufficient domestic demand.  
它所匮乏的，是足够的内需。

Yet Beijing’s response remains overwhelmingly supply-side: more bank capital, more state lending, more refinancing and more investment capacity.  
然而北京的应对举措却依然压倒性地偏向供给侧：提供更多银行资本、更多国有信贷、更多债务置换和更多投资能力。

The central government can refinance local governments. It can recapitalise banks and insurers. It can keep shifting financial burdens upward and keep the machinery functioning.  
中央政府可以为地方政府进行再融资，可以为银行和保险公司注资，可以不断将金融负担向上转移并维持机器的运转。

But it cannot solve the underlying economic problem.  
但它无法解决底层的实质性经济问题。

It cannot recapitalise demand.  
它无法为“需求”注资。
