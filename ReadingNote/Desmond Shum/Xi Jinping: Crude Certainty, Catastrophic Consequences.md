# Xi Jinping: Crude Certainty, Catastrophic Consequences
# 习近平：粗暴的确定性，灾难性的后果

**Desmond Shum**  
**沈栋**

**May 07, 2026**  
**2026年5月7日**

---

I had dinner last night with a friend who runs one of Europe’s leading think tanks.  
昨晚我和一位朋友共进晚餐，他掌管着欧洲最顶尖的智库之一。

We talked about Xi Jinping, and I shared my core assessment of him.  
我们聊到了习近平，我也分享了我对他最核心的评价。

I have long believed that Xi is an uneducated and unsophisticated man who sees himself on a historic mission to save the Chinese Communist Party.  
我长期以来一直认为，习近平是一个缺乏教育、缺乏审慎与复杂认知能力（粗疏）的人，但他却认为自己肩负着拯救中国共产党的历史使命。

Let me unpack what that means and why it matters.  
让我来拆解一下这句话的含义以及为什么它至关重要。

---

## Uneducated
## 缺乏教育

This is not an insult but a documented reality.  
这并非一种侮辱，而是有据可查的现实。

It covers his limited formal schooling, his upbringing during the Cultural Revolution, and the blunt assessment by Li Rui — Mao’s former secretary and a longtime CCP insider whose extensive diaries were donated to the Hoover Institution.  
这涵盖了他有限的正规学校教育、他在“文化大革命”期间的成长经历，以及李锐的直白评价——李锐曾任毛泽东的秘书，也是中共内部资深的知情人，其大量的日记已被捐赠给胡佛研究所。

In a late-life interview, Li Rui, who had once promoted the young Xi, remarked that he “didn’t realize [Xi’s] education level was so low — elementary school level.”  
在晚年的一次采访中，曾提拔过年轻时习近平的李锐点评道：他“没想到（习近平的）文化水平这么低——只有小学生水平”。

---

## On a Mission to Save the Party
## 怀揣拯救大党的使命

From the beginning of his rule, Xi has been both power-hungry and genuinely convinced he is the rightful inheritor of the communist founding legacy.  
从执政之初起，习近平就既对权力无比渴望，又真诚地确信自己是中共建政遗产的正统继承人。

In the later years of the Hu-Wen era, it was an open secret in Beijing that the Party was rotten to the core.  
在“胡温时代”的后期，中共体制内部已经烂透了，这在北京是一个公开的秘密。

The political system had become unsustainable — a game of musical chairs that everyone knew would eventually collapse.  
政治体制变得不可持续——成了一场每个人都知道终将崩溃的“抢椅子”击鼓传花游戏。

Xi came in determined to fix that structural decay before it destroyed the regime.  
习近平带着决心上台，誓要在这种结构性腐烂摧毁政权之前将其修复。

---

## Unsophisticated
## 粗疏与缺乏认知深度

This is the most important and under-appreciated part of the analysis.  
这是整篇分析中最重要、也最常被低估的部分。

By unsophisticated, I mean something specific: Xi lacks the political imagination to see how complex systems respond when squeezed.  
我所说的“粗疏/缺乏审慎”，有着具体的含义：习近平缺乏政治想象力，无法预判复杂系统在受到挤压和高压打击时会做出怎样的反应。

He understands command.  
他懂得下达命令。

He does not understand feedback loops.  
但他不懂得反馈回路（系统性的连锁反应）。

He can launch campaigns.  
他擅长发起运动。

He is far less capable of anticipating their second- and third-order consequences.  
但他预测这些运动所带来的二阶、三阶衍生后果的能力要逊色得多。

This is the central point: the following three examples are not meant to be exhaustive.  
这是最核心的观点：接下来的三个例子并非面面俱到。

They are illustrations of a larger pattern — an uneducated and unsophisticated mind applying campaign-style solutions to complex systems, and then producing disastrous consequences he did not foresee.  
它们是一个更宏大模式的缩影——即一个缺乏教育与复杂认知的大脑，将运动式的解决方案套用到复杂系统中，进而产生了自己未曾预料到的灾难性后果。

There are many other examples one could cite.  
人们还可以举出许多其他的例子。

But these three show the basic logic of Xi’s rule: identify a real problem, attack it with crude political force, underestimate the system-wide damage, then become trapped by the very campaign he launched.  
但仅凭这三个例子，就足以展示出习近平执政的基本逻辑：识别出一个现实存在的问题，用粗暴的政治力量去打击它，低估对整个系统造成的损害，最终被自己发起的运动彻底套牢。

---

### 1. Riding the Tiger
### 1. 骑虎难下

Once Xi began his massive anti-corruption drive in the first term — purging hundreds of thousands of thousands of officials — he could no longer get off.  
当习近平在第一任期展开大规模的反腐运动——肃清了数十万名官员之后，他就再也骑虎难下了。

The campaign has now reached into the millions.  
如今这场运动波及的人数已达数百万。

He subsequently understands that stopping would unleash a backlash strong enough to bury him, his family, and everyone associated with him.  
他随后也明白，一旦停手，释放出的反噬力量将足以埋葬他自己、他的家人以及每一个与他相关的人。

Until he finds his own “Yeltsin” who can hand power to a “Putin” and guarantee a safe retirement, he is locked in.  
除非他能找到属于自己的“叶利钦”，能够把权力交接给一个“普京”并确保他安全退休，否则他就被死死锁在了这场游戏中。

There is no peaceful exit ramp.  
这里没有和平退场的出口。

---

### 2. The Hollowing Out of the Military
### 2. 军队的空心化

Xi’s first wave of purges was about consolidating power.  
习近平的第一波肃清是为了巩固权力。

Later waves were about genuinely cleaning house.  
随后的几波则是为了真正地“清理门户”。

What he apparently did not anticipate was how deep the rot went.  
但他显然没有预料到，这其中的腐败究竟根深蒂固到了何种程度。

The People’s Liberation Army has been corrupt since the start of “reform and opening” in the late 1970s.  
中国人民解放军自20世纪70年代末“改革开放”伊始就伴随着腐败。

In my book, I recounted how, in the early 1990s, a PLA general offered to smuggle Heineken beer using military gunboats.  
在我的书中，我曾讲述过在90年代初，一位解放军将军如何提出使用军用炮艇来走私喜力啤酒。

Every senior officer who rose through the ranks over the past four decades has greased palms.  
在过去四十年里步步高升的每一位高级将领，几乎都有行贿受贿的手脚。

There are essentially no clean hands.  
本质上没有人的双手是干净的。

Xi has now conducted repeated rounds of military purges.  
习近平如今已经开展了多轮军队肃清。

Two CMC vice chairman are gone.  
两任中央军委副主席下台。

The Rocket Force was hit.  
火箭军遭受重创。

Two former defense ministers were taken down.  
两任前国防部长被查处落马。

Senior officers across the system have disappeared, been removed, or been investigated.  
整个军工和军队系统中的高层将领纷纷失踪、被撤职或接受调查。

That does not mean the PLA is harmless.  
这并不意味着解放军失去了威胁。

But it does mean any major Taiwan operation now carries much higher internal uncertainty than outside war-gamers often assume.  
但这确实意味着，任何针对台湾的大规模军事行动，其内部蕴含的不确定性都远高于外界推演推演者所假设的水平。

I do not believe Xi foresaw this level of institutional damage when he started.  
我不认为习近平在刚开始动手时，能预见到会对军队体制造成如此深重的损害。

---

### 3. Economic Consequences
### 3. 经济后果

Xi wanted to rein in the out of control real-estate sector.  
习近平想要约束失控的房地产业。

Instead, his medicine was so harsh that he largely destroyed the industry and, with it, a huge chunk of private household wealth.  
然而，他开出的药方极其猛烈，以至于几乎摧毁了这一行业，并顺带吞噬了普通民众巨额的家庭财富。

He wanted to strengthen the state sector and restore Party control over the “commanding heights” of the economy.  
他想要做强国企，恢复党对经济“制高点”的控制。

In doing so, he shattered private-sector confidence, triggering a prolonged slump in the sector that still employs most urban workers.  
然而在此过程中，他击碎了民营企业的信心，导致这一依然吸纳着绝大多数城镇劳动力就业的领域陷入长期低迷。

Today, roughly half of urban employment sits in the gig economy — no benefits, irregular income, and little security.  
如今，大约一半的城镇就业人口处于零工经济中——没有福利保障，收入不稳定，几乎没有任何安全感。

---

These are not isolated mistakes.  
这些绝非孤立的错误。

They are symptoms of the same governing mentality.  
它们是同一种执政心态与思维方式的症状。

Xi sees problems clearly enough to attack them, but not deeply enough to understand the consequences of his attack.  
习近平看清了问题所在并主动发起攻击，但他的认知深度不足以理解这种攻击所带来的后果。

He confuses force with strategy, discipline with competence, and control with stability.  
他混淆了蛮力与战略、纪律与能力、控制与稳定。

---

## In short
## 简而言之

Xi set out to become the second coming of the Great Helmsman — the man who would rescue and rejuvenate the Party.  
习近平原本立志要成为“伟大领袖”的再次降临——成为那个能够拯救并复兴伟大政党的人。

Instead, through a combination of limited education, unsophisticated judgment, and the iron logic of “riding the tiger,” he has steered China onto a far more precarious path than the one he envisioned.  
然而，在有限的教育水平、粗疏的判断力以及“骑虎难下”的铁律共同作用下，他却将中国引向了一条比他最初设想的要危险得多的道路。
