# From “Involution” to “Unworthy Forebears”: What China’s Internet Slang Reveals About Its Changing Social Psychology
# 从“内卷”到“劣祖劣宗”：中国网络流行语揭示的社会心理演变

**Desmond Shum**  
**沈栋**

**Aug 31, 2026**  
**2026年8月31日**

---

Yesterday, I came across a “Five Traps” formulation that had circulated in China around 2023.  
昨天，我偶然看到了一份大约在2023年在中国流传的“五大陷阱”说法。

I had not paid much attention to it at the time, but I found it fascinating.  
当时我并没有太注意它，但现在我觉得它耐人寻味。

So I went back and looked at some of the most representative internet phrases that have become popular in China over the past few years, and asked what their evolution might tell us about changing social psychology.  
因此，我重新梳理了过去几年在中国流行的一些最具代表性的网络词汇，并探讨它们的演变可能揭示了怎样的社会心理变化。

One of the more revealing ways to track social sentiment in China is through the words and phrases that suddenly explode online.  
在中国，观察社会情绪最能说明问题的途径之一，就是看那些突然在网络上爆红的词汇与短语。

Taken individually, many are jokes, memes or forms of self-mockery.  
单独来看，其中许多只是笑话、梗或自嘲的形式。

But when you put several years of them together, a fairly clear psychological trajectory begins to emerge.  
但当你把几年的词汇放在一起观察时，一条相当清晰的心理轨迹便开始浮现。

It is probably not a perfectly linear progression, but the pattern is still worth watching.  
这可能不是一个完美的线性演进过程，但其模式依然值得关注。

It moves from competition, to withdrawal; from disappointment, to questioning the broader environment; then toward self-protection; and now, perhaps, toward asking: Who caused all of this?  
它经历了从竞争到退出，从失望到质疑宏观环境，再到自我保护，而现在，或许正走向追问：究竟是谁造成了这一切？

---

## 2020–2021: 内卷 — “Involution”
## 2020–2021年：内卷 — “Involution”

One of the first phrases to go truly mainstream was 内卷, usually translated as “involution.”  
最早真正走向主流的词汇之一是“内卷”，通常翻译为“involution”。

It described a familiar condition:  
它描述了一种人们十分熟悉的状态：

Everyone works harder.  
每个人都更加努力。

Working hours get longer.  
工作时间变得更长。

Children study more intensely.  
孩子们学习得更加卷疯狂。

Competition becomes fiercer.  
竞争变得愈发激烈。

Yet the actual rewards keep shrinking.  
然而实际的回报却在不断缩水。

It was not that opportunities had disappeared entirely.  
这倒不是说机会完全消失了。

Rather, more and more people were asking:  
而是越来越多的人在问：

Why am I running faster and faster without actually moving forward?  
为什么我跑得越来越快，实际上却没有向前移动？

At this stage, the psychology was still fundamentally competitive.  
在这个阶段，人们的心理本质上仍是讲求竞争的。

People had not given up on the game.  
人们并没有放弃这场游戏。

They were beginning to question why the game demanded ever more effort for ever less reward.  
他们只是开始质疑，为什么这场游戏需要付出越来越多的努力，换来的回报却越来越少。

---

## 2021: 躺平 — “Lying Flat”
## 2021年：躺平 — “Lying Flat”

Then came 躺平 — “lying flat.”  
紧接着出现的便是“躺平”。

If greater effort produces diminishing returns, then perhaps the rational response is simply to stop competing so aggressively.  
如果付出的努力越大带来的边际收益越低，那么或许最理性的应对方式就是直接停止如此激烈的竞争。

Work enough to get by.  
工作够维持生计就行。

Consume less.  
减少消费。

Want less.  
降低欲望。

Lower expectations.  
降低预期。

Refuse the endless race.  
拒绝无休止的角逐。

What made 躺平 interesting was not laziness.  
让“躺平”变得耐人寻味的并非懒惰。

It represented a psychological shift:  
它代表了一种心理上的转变：

from “I need to work harder.”  
从“我需要更加努力”；

to “Why should I keep working this hard?”  
转变为“我为什么还要继续这么拼命？”

That was already beginning to undermine one of the most important social beliefs of the reform era:  
这已经开始动摇改革开放时期最重要的社会信念之一：

Sacrifice today so that tomorrow will be better.  
牺牲今天，以便明天会更好。

Once people begin to doubt that tomorrow will necessarily be better, the meaning of effort itself starts to change.  
一旦人们开始怀疑明天是否一定会更好，努力本身的意义也就开始发生改变。

---

## 2023: 孔乙己文学 — “Kong Yiji Literature”
## 2023年：孔乙己文学 — “Kong Yiji Literature”

By 2023, a sharper expression emerged: 孔乙己文学 — “Kong Yiji literature.”  
到了2023年，一种更为犀利的表达浮现了：“孔乙己文学”。

Kong Yiji, in Lu Xun’s story, is an impoverished scholar who still cannot bring himself to take off the long gown associated with an educated man.  
在鲁迅的小说中，孔乙己是一个贫困潦倒却依然脱不下象征读书人长衫的穷书生。

Young graduates began describing their own qualifications as: “Kong Yiji’s long gown that cannot be taken off.”  
年轻的毕业生们开始将自己的学历和文凭形容为：“脱不下的孔乙己长衫”。

The psychology behind this was more complicated than simply “graduate unemployment.”  
这背后的心理比单纯的“大学生失业”要更为复杂。

This generation had grown up being told:  
这一代人从小被告知：

Study hard.  
好好学习。

Get into a good university.  
考个好大学。

Earn qualifications.  
拿到好文凭。

That is the path to upward mobility.  
这就是通往阶层上升的途径。

But as more graduates discovered that they could no longer find jobs matching their education, investment and expectations, the response they encountered was effectively:  
然而，随着越来越多的毕业生发现他们再也找不到与自己的教育背景、投入和预期相匹配的工作时，他们得到的社会回应实质上却是：

Stop being so picky.  
别那么挑剔了。

Take off Kong Yiji’s gown.  
把孔乙己的长衫脱下来吧。

The younger generation’s response was powerful:  
年轻一代的反驳非常强力：

I did not insist on putting this gown on by myself.  
这件长衫不是我自己硬要穿上的。

Society told me from childhood that I had to wear it.  
是这个社会从小告诉我必须穿上它。

Now that the promise no longer works, why am I being blamed for refusing to take it off?  
现在承诺兑现不了了，为什么反过来怪我不肯脱掉它？

So “Kong Yiji literature” represented a deeper form of disappointment:  
因此，“孔乙己文学”代表了一种更深层次的失望：

Not only was competition producing less reward; the promised route of social mobility itself appeared to be breaking down.  
不仅竞争产生的回报在减少，就连原本承诺的阶层流动通道本身似乎也在坍塌。

This was no longer simply a complaint about competition.  
这不再仅仅是对竞争的抱怨。

It was the beginning of doubt about the social bargain itself.  
这是对整个社会契约本身产生怀疑的开端。

---

## 2023: The “Five Traps”
## 2023年：五大陷阱 — “Five Traps”

At roughly the same time, among intellectuals, people in finance, and the more educated urban classes, another formulation circulated: China was facing the “Five Traps.”  
几乎在同一时期，在知识分子、金融界人士以及受教育程度较高的城市阶层中，另一种说法开始流传：中国正面临“五大陷阱”。

Politically, the Thucydides Trap.  
政治上：修昔底德陷阱（Thucydides Trap）。

In consumption, a Keynesian liquidity trap.  
消费上：凯恩斯流动性陷阱（liquidity trap）。

In property investment, a Minsky moment.  
房地产投资上：明斯基时刻（Minsky moment）。

Demographically, the Lewis turning point.  
人口结构上：刘易斯拐点（Lewis turning point）。

Socially, the Tacitus Trap.  
社会治理上：塔西佗陷阱（Tacitus Trap）。

The concepts themselves are debatable.  
这些概念本身是否成立尚存争议。

What matters more is the psychological shift behind them.  
更重要的是其背后所反映的心理转变。

“Kong Yiji literature” asked: Why is my own path becoming harder?  
“孔乙己文学”在问：为什么我自己的路变得越来越难走？

The “Five Traps” said: The whole country may now be facing simultaneous structural problems.  
而“五大陷阱”则指出：整个国家现在可能正同时面临着结构性难题。

Individual frustration was beginning to be interpreted as part of a much larger structural predicament.  
个人的挫败感开始被解读为某种更庞大的结构性困境的一部分。

---

## 2024: 历史的垃圾时间 — “The Garbage Time of History”
## 2024年：历史的垃圾时间 — “The Garbage Time of History”

By 2024, the language became noticeably darker.  
到了2024年，网络语言变得明显更加阴郁。

The most striking phrase was: 历史的垃圾时间 — “the garbage time of history.”  
最令人震撼的词汇莫过于：“历史的垃圾时间”。

“Garbage time” is a sporting term.  
“垃圾时间”原本是一个体育术语。

It describes the final stage of a game when the result is already decided and the remaining minutes can no longer change the outcome.  
它用来形容比赛的最后阶段——胜负已定，剩下的时间再怎么打也无法改变结果。

Applied to history and society, the implication is bleak:  
套用到历史和社会上，其寓意是相当苍凉的：

Perhaps this is no longer just another economic downturn that will recover in a few years.  
或许这不再仅仅是过几年就会复苏的又一次经济下行。

Perhaps the direction itself has changed.  
或许方向本身已经改变了。

The phrase implied: Perhaps there is no longer very much that can be reversed.  
这个词暗示着：或许已经没有多少大局是可以大势逆转的了。

The psychology had moved beyond identifying structural problems.  
人们的心理已经超越了识别结构性问题的阶段。

It was becoming pessimism about the trajectory itself.  
正演变成对发展轨迹本身的大局悲观。

---

## 2024: 牛马 and 班味 — “Cattle and Horses” and “The Smell of Work”
## 2024年：牛马与班味 — “Cattle and Horses” and “The Smell of Work”

At the same time, macro pessimism became increasingly personal.  
与此同时，宏观上的悲观情绪变得越来越个人化。

牛马 — literally “cattle and horses” became a common way for workers to describe themselves.  
“牛马”——字面意思就是牛和马，成为了打工人用来自嘲的常用词。

Not “builders of the nation.”  
不再是“国家建设者”。

Not “strivers.”  
不再是“奋斗者”。

Not even simply “workers.”  
甚至不再仅仅是“劳动者”。

But beasts of burden: used, driven and exhausted.  
而是变成了牛马牲畜：被使用、被驱使、被榨干。

Then came 班味 — literally “the smell of work.”  
紧接着出现了“班味”。

It describes the physical and emotional residue of long-term working life: tired eyes, drained energy, loss of vitality, the sense that someone carries the smell of the office on their entire body.  
它描述了长期打工生活留下的生理与心理痕迹：疲倦的眼神、耗尽的精神、活力的丧失，以及一个人全身都散发着办公室气味的感觉。

And its opposite became another popular aspiration: 松弛感 — a sense of ease.  
而它的反义词则成为了另一种广受欢迎的向往：“松弛感”。

That contrast is revealing.  
这种对比极具启发性。

During China’s high-growth years, the ambition was often: I want to get rich.  
在中国的高高速增长年份，人们的野心往往是：我想发财。

Later: I want a successful career.  
后来是：我想事业有成。

Then increasingly: I just want work to stop consuming my whole life.  
再到后来日益变成：我只想让工作别再啃噬我的整个生活。

The aspiration itself was getting smaller.  
愿望本身正在变得越来越微小。

---

## 2025: 浪浪山 — “Langlang Mountain”
## 2025年：浪浪山 — “Langlang Mountain”

By 2025, 浪浪山 — “Langlang Mountain” became one of the most revealing social metaphors.  
到了2025年，“浪浪山”成为了最具说服力的社会隐喻之一。

The little demons of Nobody / 《浪浪山小妖怪》 are not heroes.  
《中国奇谭》之《小妖怪的夏天》里的浪浪山小妖怪们并不是英雄。

They exist at the bottom of a vast hierarchy.  
他们处于庞大等级制度的最底层。

They work hard. They follow orders. They try to improve their circumstances.  
他们努力工作，听从指挥，试图改善自己的处境。

But their ability to control their own fate is extremely limited.  
但他们掌控自己命运的能力却极其有限。

So one line resonated immediately: “I want to leave Langlang Mountain.” (我想离开浪浪山。)  
因此，有一句话立刻引起了强烈共鸣：“我想离开浪浪山。”

And then came the more pessimistic response: “But where isn’t Langlang Mountain?” (何处不是浪浪山？)  
随之而来的是更为悲观的回应：“何处不是浪浪山？”

This was no longer simply: My boss is bad. I should change companies.  
这不再仅仅是：我的老板不好，我应该换家公司。

It became: Perhaps the problem is not this company. Wherever I go, my place within the hierarchy may be much the same.  
它变成了：或许问题不在于这家公司；无论我走到哪里，我在等级秩序中的位置可能都相差无几。

This is a deeper form of helplessness than 躺平.  
这是一种比“躺平”更深层的无力感。

Lying flat at least implies: I choose not to participate.  
“躺平”至少还意味着：“我选择不参与。”

Langlang Mountain begins to suggest: I do not even know where else I can go, because there may be no real exit.  
“浪浪山”则开始暗示：“我甚至不知道还能去哪里，因为可能根本没有真正的出口。”

---

## 2025: 匆匆忙忙，连滚带爬 — “Rushing Around, Stumbling and Scrambling”
## 2025年：匆匆忙忙，连滚带爬 — “Rushing Around, Stumbling and Scrambling”

The same year, another line became enormously popular:  
同一年，另一句话引发了巨大的流行：

本来应该从从容容、游刃有余，现在是匆匆忙忙、连滚带爬。  
“本来应该从从容容、游刃有余，现在是匆匆忙忙、连滚带爬。”

It can obviously be used to describe individual life.  
这句话显然可以用来形容个人的生活。

But its wider resonance in China is more interesting: How did such a strong hand end up being played like this?  
但它在中国引起的更广泛共鸣却更为耐人寻味：一手好牌，怎么就打成了这样？

China once appeared to possess:  
中国曾经似乎拥有：

the dividends of globalization,  
全球化的红利、

massive foreign investment,  
海量的外资流、

a demographic dividend,  
人口红利、

a property boom,  
房地产繁荣、

manufacturing dominance,  
制造业的主导地位、

enormous fiscal resources,  
庞大的财政资源、

and a vast domestic market.  
以及广阔的国内市场。

Yet only a few years later it was simultaneously facing:  
然而仅仅几年后，它却同时面临着：

a property crisis,  
房地产危机、

local-government debt,  
地方政府债务、

weak consumption,  
消费疲软、

employment pressure,  
就业压力、

population decline,  
人口负增长、

weak private-sector confidence,  
民营企业信心不足、

and a worsening external environment.  
以及日益恶化的外部环境。

So the question began to shift from: What is happening? toward: How did things end up like this?  
因此，人们的问题开始从“发生了什么？”转向“事情是怎么变成这样的？”

That is a subtle but important psychological change.  
这是一个微妙但至关重要的心理变化。

The issue is no longer only the result.  
焦点不再仅仅是结果。

It begins to involve judgment, decision-making and competence.  
它开始涉及对判断、决策和能力的质疑。

---

## 2026: 爱你老己 — “Love Ya, Old Self”
## 2026年：爱你老己 — “Love Ya, Old Self”

By 2026, some of the language began to turn inward.  
到了2026年，一些网络语言开始转向内部。

One example is: 爱你老己 — roughly, “Love ya, old self.”  
一个例子就是：“爱你老己”。

It is a playful way of treating oneself almost like an old friend.  
这是一种幽默自嘲地对待自己、把自己当成老朋友的方式。

On the surface, it is light and humorous. But the psychology behind it is quite clear:  
表面上看，它轻松幽默，但其背后的心理却非常清晰：

If I cannot control the outside environment, I can at least protect myself.  
如果我无法控制外部环境，我至少可以保护好自己。

The emphasis used to be on changing one’s fate.  
过去，人们强调的是改变命运。

Increasingly, it is on protecting: one’s emotions, one’s time, one’s body, one’s boundaries.  
而现在，人们日益强调的是保护：自己的情绪、自己的时间、自己的身体、自己的边界。

---

## 2026: 前额叶友好 — “Prefrontal-Cortex Friendly”
## 2026年：前额叶友好 — “Prefrontal-Cortex Friendly”

Another interesting phrase is: 前额叶友好 — “prefrontal-cortex friendly.”  
另一个有趣的词汇是：“前额叶友好”。

In plain English: Stop exhausting my brain.  
通俗来说就是：别再消耗我的脑子了。

People want work to be simpler, relationships to be simpler, life to contain less conflict, fewer decisions and less emotional consumption.  
人们希望工作更简单，人际关系更简单，生活少一点冲突、少一点决策，少一点情绪消耗。

This is no longer quite the psychology of 躺平.  
这与“躺平”的心理已不尽相同。

Lying flat said: I refuse to play.  
“躺平”说的是：“我不玩了。”

“Prefrontal-cortex friendly” says: I will still play, but I want to minimize how much the game takes out of me.  
“前额叶友好”说的则是：“我还会继续玩，但我希望把这场游戏对我的内耗降到最低。”

That is no longer rebellion.  
这不再是反抗。

It is adaptation.  
这是适应。

From the perspective of social psychology, that shift may be more important than lying flat itself.  
从社会心理学的角度来看，这种转变可能比“躺平”本身更为重要。

---

## August 2026: 劣祖劣宗 — “Unworthy Forebears”
## 2026年8月：劣祖劣宗 — “Unworthy Forebears”

In recent weeks, a much harsher phrase has begun circulating: 劣祖劣宗 — “unworthy forebears.”  
近几周来，一个严厉得多的词汇开始流传：“劣祖劣宗”。

It is a mocking inversion of 列祖列宗, the traditional expression for one’s honored ancestors.  
它是对传统中表达敬重的“列祖列宗”一词的讽刺性颠覆。

The accusation behind it is direct: You enjoyed the dividends. We inherited the bill.  
其背后的控诉十分直接：你们享受了红利，我们继承了账单。

The previous generation experienced: rising property prices, rapid economic growth, expanding employment, asset appreciation, and social mobility.  
上一代人经历的是：房价上涨、经济快速增长、就业机会扩大、资产升值以及阶层向上流动。

What younger Chinese increasingly see instead is: high housing costs and mortgages, employment difficulties, an aging population, pension burdens, more intense competition, and lower expectations for the future.  
而年轻一代中国人看到的却日益变成了：高昂的房价与房贷、就业困难、人口老龄化、养老金负担、更激烈的竞争以及对未来更低的预期。

So 劣祖劣宗 introduces something relatively unusual in the earlier vocabulary: the attribution of responsibility.  
因此，“劣祖劣宗”引入了此前网络流行语中相对罕见的东西：责任的归咎。

## Put the Terms Together
## 总结这些词汇

The trajectory becomes quite interesting:  
这条演进轨迹变得相当耐人寻味：

* **2020–2021: 内卷 — Involution**  
  Why am I working harder and finding it increasingly difficult to move upward?  
  为什么我越来越努力，却发现越来越难向上阶层跃升？

* **2021: 躺平 — Lying Flat**  
  If that is the case, why should I keep trying so hard?  
  既然如此，我为什么还要这么拼命？

* **2023: 孔乙己文学 — Kong Yiji Literature**  
  The promised route to upward mobility is beginning to fail.  
  原本承诺的上升通道正在失效。

* **2023: 五大陷阱 — The Five Traps**  
  Perhaps this is not an individual problem, but a structural one.  
  或许这并非个人问题，而是结构性问题。

* **2024: 历史的垃圾时间 — The Garbage Time of History**  
  Perhaps this is not even temporary. Perhaps the trajectory itself has changed.  
  或许这甚至不是暂时的，或许是时代轨迹本身已经改变。

* **2024: 牛马 / 班味 — Cattle and Horses / The Smell of Work**  
  I increasingly feel like labor that is simply being consumed.  
  我越来越感觉自己只是被纯粹消耗的劳动力。

* **2025: 浪浪山 — Langlang Mountain**  
  I am trapped inside a hierarchy I cannot control.  
  我被困在一个我无法控制的等级体制内部。

## What Matters Is Not Simply That People Are Becoming More Pessimistic
## 关键不仅仅在于人们变得越来越悲观

What I find interesting about this progression is that it does not simply suggest that pessimism is getting worse.  
我觉得这个演进过程最耐人寻味的地方在于，它不仅仅表明悲观情绪在加剧。

More importantly, pessimism appears to be becoming increasingly internalized and personalized—and may now be beginning to search for responsibility.  
更重要的是，这种悲观情绪似乎正在日益内化与个人化——而且现在可能正开始寻找责任方。

At first, people complained about: too much competition.  
起初，人们抱怨的是：竞争太激烈。

Then: the rules of competition.  
接着是：竞争的规则。

Then: the failure of the old economic and social bargain.  
再接着是：旧有经济与社会契约的失效。

Then: whether the country’s broader development trajectory itself had gone wrong.  
然后是：国家整体的发展轨迹本身是否出了问题。

After that, people increasingly stopped expecting the environment to improve quickly and turned instead toward: protecting themselves, lowering expectations, reducing risk, conserving energy, and seeking stability.  
在此之后，人们日益不再指望环境能迅速好转，转而寻求：自我保护、降低预期、防范风险、保存精力以及追求稳定。

That shift matters.  
这种转变至关重要。

Because once pessimism begins to change behavior, it is no longer merely “sentiment.”  
因为一旦悲观情绪开始改变人们的行为，它就不再仅仅是一种“情绪”。

People take fewer risks.  
人们减少冒风险。

They save more.  
他们存更多的钱。

They consume less.  
他们减少消费。

They hesitate to buy property.  
他们在买房上犹豫不决。

They become less willing to start businesses.  
他们创业的意愿降低。

They postpone marriage.  
他们推迟结婚。

They have fewer children.  
他们少生甚至不生孩子。

They seek security rather than opportunity.  
他们追求安全感而非机会。

In other words: The social mood begins to reinforce the economic conditions that produced it.  
换句话说：社会情绪开始反过来加剧造就这种情绪的经济现状。

And if expressions such as 劣祖劣宗 continue to develop, the next stage may be different again.  
而如果像“劣祖劣宗”这样的表达继续发酵，下一个阶段可能又会大不相同。

For several years, people have essentially been asking: How do I survive in this environment?  
几年来，人们实质上一直在问：我该如何在这样的环境中生存？

The next question may increasingly become: Why did the environment become like this?  
下一个问题可能将日益变成：环境为什么会变成这样？

And after that comes the question most worth watching: Who made it this way?  
而在那之后，最值得关注的问题便随之而来：是谁把它变成这样的？

This progression is probably not perfectly linear.  
这种演进可能并非完美的线性过程。

But as a way of tracking how social psychology in China is evolving, I think it is well worth watching.  
但作为观察中国社会心理如何演进的一种视角，我认为它非常值得持续关注。
