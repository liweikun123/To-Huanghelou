# Xi Jinping and the Slow Corrosion of China
**习近平与中国漫长的腐蚀**

**Desmond Shum**  
**沈栋**  

**Sep 05, 2026**  
**2026年9月5日**

## The Slow Corrosion of China’s Governing System
**中国治理体系的漫长腐蚀**

For years now, I have increasingly come to believe that the most likely course of China’s development is not collapse. It is continued decay.  
多年来，我越来越相信，中国发展最可能的走向并非崩溃。而是持续的衰变。

China has developed extraordinary strengths: enormous industrial capacity, extensive infrastructure, a vast pool of relatively cheap labour, highly capable engineers, a huge domestic market and an unbridled state with an administrative reach impossible to replicate in any civil society. None of that is disappearing overnight.  
中国已经具备了非凡的优势：庞大的工业产能，广泛的基础设施，庞大且相对廉价的劳动力资源，极具能力的工程师队伍，巨大的国内市场，以及一个拥有任何公民社会都无法复制的行政触角的无所约束的国家机器。这一切都不会在宵夜之间消失。

But beneath those strengths, many of the mechanisms that allow a country to adjust, correct mistakes and renew itself are steadily weakening. Collapse is visible. Corrosion is harder to see. A tree can keep standing long after rot has set into its roots. It may still put out new branches and appear formidable from a distance. But the withering has already begun, and the damage becomes harder to conceal over time. I increasingly see China in those terms.  
但在这些优势之下，许多允许一个国家进行自我调整、纠正错误和自我更新的机制正在稳步削弱。崩溃是显而易见的。腐蚀却更难察觉。一棵树在根部腐烂后很久，依然可以维持挺立。它可能还会长出新枝，远看依然显得不可撼动。但枯萎已经开始，随着时间的推移，这种损伤变得越来越难以掩盖。我越来越倾向于从这个角度来看待中国。

The deterioration comes from three different sources. First, there is damage caused directly by policies introduced under Xi Jinping. Second, the governing system itself is increasingly unable to adapt: partly because it remains trapped in habits formed during the high-growth era, and partly because deeper reforms now collide with the fundamental interests of the Party-state. Third, the underlying conditions that supported China’s rise—demographics, urbanisation, property demand, debt capacity, easy technological catch-up and a highly accommodating external environment—are themselves changing. These are different problems, but the danger is that they are now reinforcing one another.  
这种恶化来自于三个不同的源头。首先是习近平主导推出的政策直接造成的破坏。其次，治理体系本身越来越难以适应变化：部分原因是它仍然深陷高增长时代形成的习惯中，部分原因是更深层次的改革如今与党国体制的根本利益发生了冲突。第三，支撑中国崛起的底层条件——人口结构、城镇化、房地产需求、债务承受能力、容易实现的技术追赶，以及极具包容性的外部环境——它们本身也正在发生改变。这些是不同的问题，但危险在于，它们现在正处于相互强化的状态。

## I. Xi’s Reversal
## 一、 习近平倒退

Some of China’s present difficulties are not inevitable consequences of ageing, economic maturity or slower growth. They are the result of political choices.  
中国目前面临的一些困难，并不是人口老龄化、经济成熟或增速放缓的必然结果。它们是政治选择的产物。

The most consequential change under Xi Jinping has been a reversal in the broad direction China had followed since Mao. The post-Mao system remained a dictatorship, but it gradually expanded economic and social space, encouraged decentralisation and experimentation, integrated with the world and developed stronger rules around leadership and succession. Xi has reversed that direction across several fronts.  
习近平任内最具影响力的变化，是对毛泽东时代以来中国所遵循的大方向的倒退。后毛泽东时代的体系虽然依然是专政体制，但它逐渐扩大了经济和社会空间，鼓励地方分权和政策试点，积极融入世界，并围绕领导权与接班制度建立了更严密的规则。而习近平在多个领域倒转了这一方向。

### From institutionalisation back toward personal rule
### 从制度化倒退回个人统治

The post-Mao leadership developed imperfect but meaningful norms around collective leadership, succession, retirement and the distribution of authority. Xi has systematically dismantled them. Power is increasingly concentrated around one person, institutional restraints have eroded and succession has become completely unpredictable.  
后毛泽东时代的领导层围绕集体领导、接班、退休和权力分配，建立了虽不完美但具有实际意义的规范。习近平系统性地拆毁了这些规范。权力日益高度集中于一人之手，制度约束被侵蚀，接班问题变得完全不可预测。

This is not simply a change in personnel. It is a shift from a dictatorship that was slowly institutionalising itself toward one increasingly dependent on a single leader’s authority and judgment.  
这不仅仅是人事上的变动。这是一场深刻的转变：从一个原本正在缓慢推进自我制度化的专政体制，转向了一个日益依赖单一领导人的权威与个人判断的体制。

### From decentralised experimentation toward command from the centre
### 从地方试点倒退回中央集权指令

Reform-era China drew much of its adaptability from local experimentation. Provinces competed, officials tested policies and successful experiments could be adopted nationally while failures remained local.  
改革开放时代的中国，其大部分适应能力源于地方的探索试验。各省份展开竞争，官员们测试新政，成功的试点经验可以在全国推广，而失败的代价则被控制在局部。

Xi has moved decisions upward. Political conformity is now supreme, local discretion has narrowed, and officials are increasingly expected to anticipate the preferences of the top leadership rather than test alternatives from below. Central command may produce greater uniformity, but it reduces the system’s capacity to discover solutions from below.  
习近平将决策权全面上收。政治顺从如今被置于至高无上的地位，地方的自主裁量权被大幅压缩，官员们越来越被要求去揣摩高层领导的偏好，而不是从基层去测试替代方案。中央发号施令或许能带来更高程度的统一，但它却削弱了该体系由下而上探索解决问题的能力。

### From pragmatism toward ideological control and regime preservation
### 从实用主义倒退回意识形态控制与政权维护

The reform era increasingly judged policies by their contribution to growth, investment, employment and national strength. Under Xi, political loyalty, ideological conformity and preservation of Party rule intrude much more deeply into economic and social decision-making.  
改革开放时代越来越倾向于根据政策对经济增长、投资、就业和综合国力的贡献来评估其好坏。而在习近平统治下，政治忠诚、意识形态对齐以及维护党的统治，以更深的程度干预到了经济与社会的决策之中。

What Beijing describes as “national security” now extends across technology, finance, data, education, business, foreign contacts and ordinary commercial activity. The Party increasingly treats independent economic, social and international activity as a potential political vulnerability. Economic efficiency must therefore coexist with political obedience, information control, reduced foreign dependence and protection of the regime from perceived threats.  
北京所定义的“国家安全”，如今已延伸至科技、金融、数据、教育、商业、对外接触以及日常商业活动的方方面面。党越来越将独立的经济、社会和国际活动视为潜在的政治漏洞。因此，经济效率必须与政治服从、信息管控、减少对外依赖以及保护政权免受潜在威胁共存。

### From expanding market space toward deeper political penetration of business
### 从拓展市场空间倒退回政治对企业的深度渗透

China’s rise depended heavily on private initiative. The Party retained ultimate political control, but entrepreneurs operated in areas where the state often stepped back, making private companies major sources of employment, innovation and wealth creation.  
中国的崛起高度依赖于民营领域的积极性与主动性。党虽然保留了最终的政治控制权，但企业家们在国家退让出的领域中深耕，使民营企业成为了就业、创新和财富创造的主要源泉。

Under Xi, political supervision has expanded inside business and entire industries have been reshaped by campaigns. Entrepreneurs have learned that political correctness outranks commercial success—and that commercial success offers no protection if they get the politics wrong.  
在习近平任内，政治监督在企业内部不断扩大，整个行业因一场场整顿运动而被重塑。企业家们已经明白，政治正确高于商业成功——而且如果他们在政治方向上犯了错，商业上的成功无法提供任何保护。

Long-term investment requires reasonably predictable political and legal boundaries. Under Xi, those boundaries have repeatedly shifted toward greater political intrusion, shortening investment horizons and increasing risk aversion. Verbal declarations of support for the private sector cannot reverse expectations built over more than a decade.  
长期投资需要相对可预测的政治与法律边界。但在习近平统治下，这些边界一再向着政治进一步干预的方向移动，从而缩短了投资周期，并加剧了避险情绪。口头上对民营经济表示支持的声明，无法扭转十多年来建立起的既有预期。

### From international integration toward deliberate insulation from external dependence
### 从国际融合倒退回刻意隔绝外部依赖

China’s rise was inseparable from foreign capital, technology, expertise, multinational companies and global markets. Under Xi, dependence itself has increasingly been treated as a strategic weakness.  
中国的崛起与外资、技术、专业知识、跨国公司以及全球市场密不可分。而在习近平统治下，这种依赖本身越来越被视为一种战略上的弱点。

The government seeks to reduce reliance on foreign technology, supply chains, capital and institutions wherever that reliance might constrain its freedom of political action. China still needs foreign markets, capital, technology and trade, but the strategic question has changed—from how to benefit more from integration to how to retain those benefits while reducing the leverage outsiders gain from interdependence. China’s own policies have also made governments, companies, institutions and investors less willing to deepen their exposure.  
凡是依赖可能约束其政治行动自由的地方，政府都试图减少对外国技术、供应链、资金和机构的依赖。中国依然需要外国市场、资金、技术和贸易，但战略层面的核心问题已经改变——从“如何从融合中获取更多利益”，变成了“如何在保留这些利益的同时，减少外人因相互依赖而获得的杠杆工具”。而中国自身的政策，也使得外国政府、企业、机构和投资者不再那么愿意深化对其风险敞口。

---

Taken together, these reversals move China away from institutionalisation, decentralisation, pragmatism, market autonomy and deep international integration, and toward personal rule, central command, ideological control, political penetration of business and insulation from external dependence.  
总而言之，这些倒退正在将中国推离制度化、地方分权、实用主义、市场自主权和深度国际融合，并推向个人统治、中央集权指令、意识形态控制、政治渗透商业以及隔绝外部依赖。

These reversals are not simply ideological impulses. They reflect a political calculation. The reform era created forces that were economically productive but increasingly difficult for the Party to control: powerful private capital, more autonomous local officials, deeper foreign dependence, expanding intellectual and social space and institutional norms that placed some limits on the top leadership. Xi’s response has been to pull those forces back under political control.  
这些倒退不仅仅是意识形态上的冲动。它们反映出一种政治算计。改革开放时代创造出了在经济上极具生产力、但对党而言却越来越难以控制的力量：强大的民营资本、更具自主性的地方官员、更深的对外依赖、不断扩大的思想与社会空间，以及对最高领导层形成一定约束的制度规范。习近平的回应，就是将这些力量重新收归政治控制之下。

From the standpoint of economic development, many of these reversals are damaging. From the standpoint of protecting the Party’s monopoly on power and strengthening the authority of the centre, they have their own logic. That conflict is central to what follows.  
从经济发展的角度来看，许多此类倒退都是具有破坏性的。但从维护党的权力垄断和加强中央权威的角度来看，它们有其自身的逻辑。这种冲突，正是后续讨论的核心所在。

## II. The Limits of the Governing System
## 二、 治理体系的极限

The second problem is different. China’s governing system is running into two limits at once.  
第二个问题有所不同。中国的治理体系正同时撞上两个极限。

The first is inertia. Many of the methods that once made the system successful were formed when China was poor, underbuilt, rapidly urbanising and growing at extraordinary speed. The second is more fundamental. Some of the changes China now needs would weaken the institutions, resource flows and discretionary powers on which the Party-state itself depends. At that point, the obstacle is no longer simply habit. It is political interest.  
第一个是惯性。许多曾经让该体系取得成功的做法，都是在中国尚处于贫困、基础设施欠缺、快速城镇化以及以非凡速度增长的时期形成的。第二个极限则更加本质。中国现在需要的一些改变，将会削弱党国体制本身所依赖的制度、资源流动以及裁量权力。到了那个地步，障碍就不再仅仅是习惯了。而是政治利益。

### The habit of postponing bad outcomes
### 延后不良后果的习惯

For decades, Chinese policymakers learned a powerful lesson: if a problem can be prevented from exploding today, economic growth may make it manageable tomorrow. That lesson was not irrational.  
几十年来，中国的政策制定者学会了一个深刻的教训：如果一个问题今天能被阻止爆发，经济增长或许能在明天让它变得可控。那个教训在当时并非毫无道理。

Consider bank bad debts. China’s banking system accumulated enormous bad-loan problems in the 1990s. The government did not force the system to recognise and liquidate those losses in a timely fashion. Bad assets were shifted, extended, recapitalised and absorbed. Then the economy grew rapidly. Government revenues expanded, asset values rose and new profits accumulated. Problems that once appeared enormous became smaller relative to the economy surrounding them.  
以银行不良贷款为例。中国的银行体系在20世纪90年代积累了巨大的不良贷款问题。政府并没有强制银行体系及时确认和清算这些损失。不良资产被转移、延期、注资重组并消化。随后，经济迎来了高速增长。政府财政收入扩大，资产价值上涨，新的利润不断积累。曾经看似庞大的问题，在宏观经济总量的映衬下变得微不足道。

Variations of the same approach appeared in state-owned enterprises, local-government borrowing, infrastructure and property. A governing instinct developed: suppress it, stabilise it, extend the maturity, roll over the loan, buy time. For much of the reform era, it worked remarkably well.  
类似方法的变体也出现在国有企业、地方政府借贷、基础设施和房地产领域。一种治理本能随之形成：压制它、稳定它、展期债务、滚动贷款、拖延时间。在改革开放的大部分时期，这种做法效果显著。

But the model contained a hidden assumption: time solves the problem only if the economy is growing fast enough. Today, delay plus low growth means the problem accumulates. A governing technique born in an era of rapid growth has survived after the condition that made it effective disappeared. The technique became habit. Now the habit is becoming disease.  
但该模式包含了一个隐性假设：只有在经济增长足够快的情况下，时间才能解决问题。如今，拖延加上低增长，意味着问题的不断堆积。诞生于高速增长时代的某种治理手段，在其发挥功效的前提条件消失后依然存续了下来。手段变成了习惯。而如今，这种习惯正在演变成病疾。

---

Real estate is a clear example. The bursting of China’s property bubble was not avoidable forever. The country had built too much housing, developers had taken on excessive leverage and the growth model had become dangerously dependent on land and property. Some adjustment was inevitable. The question was how the losses would be dealt with.  
房地产就是一个明证。中国房地产泡沫的破裂是不可能永远避免的。这个国家建造了过多的住房，开发商承担了过高的杠杆，而增长模式对土地和房地产产生了危险的依赖。某种程度的调整是不可避免的。问题在于，这些损失将如何被处理。

A genuine restructuring requires losses to be recognised. Developers fail, creditors take losses, assets are repriced, projects move to stronger owners and banks recapitalise where necessary. China has instead repeatedly tried to slow that process. Loans are extended, projects are kept on life support, insolvent developers linger, local governments intervene and price adjustment is administered.  
一场真正的重组需要确认损失。开发商倒闭、债权人承担损失、资产重新定价、项目转移至实力更强的持有者手中，银行则在必要时补充资本。然而，中国却一再试图放缓这一进程。贷款被展期、项目靠人工输血维持生命、已无偿付能力的开发商继续苟延残喘、地方政府强势干预，价格调整则受到行政管制。

The objective is understandable: preserve an orderly unwinding. But slowing the recognition of a loss does not eliminate it. It spreads the loss through time.  
其目的可以理解：维持一场秩序井然的清算过程。但推迟确认损失并不能消除损失。它只是将损失摊薄摊长到了时间长河中。

The same pattern exists in local-government finance. Debt is rolled over, refinanced or exchanged for longer-dated obligations. But refinancing is not restructuring. Changing the maturity of a bad investment does not make the investment good.  
同样的模式也存在于地方政府财政中。债务被展期、再融资，或是置换为更长期限的债券。但再融资并不等同于结构性重组。改变一项糟糕投资的还款期限，并不能让这项投资本身变好。

---

Education may now be absorbing some of the same logic. When employment becomes difficult, expanding undergraduate, master’s and doctoral enrolment delays entry into the workforce. If higher education increasingly functions as a holding area for young people whom the economy cannot productively employ, the employment problem has simply been pushed several years into the future.  
教育领域如今可能也在套用某种同样的逻辑。当就业变得困难时，扩大本科、硕士和博士的招生规模，便推迟了年轻人进入劳动力市场的时间。如果高等教育日益演变成一个用来安置经济无法有效吸纳的年轻人的蓄水池，那么就业问题不过是被推迟到了几年之后。

---

Under Xi, this old habit acquires an additional political logic. In the past, delay was often justified by the expectation that growth would eventually absorb the problem. Today, delay also helps officials avoid responsibility. A developer bankruptcy means someone must acknowledge that loans are unrecoverable. A local-government restructuring requires someone to decide who takes the loss. Closing an unsuccessful industrial project requires admitting that the original investment failed. Reversing a policy may imply that the policy itself was wrong.  
在习近平统治下，这种旧习惯又叠加了一层政治逻辑。过去，拖延往往能借由“增长最终会消化问题”的预期而获得合理性。而今天，拖延还能帮助官员规避责任。一家开发商破产，意味着必须有人承认贷款已无法收回。地方政府债务重组，需要有人决定由谁来承担损失。关闭一个失败的工业项目，需要承认最初的投资彻底搞砸了。纠正一项政策，可能暗示着该政策本身就是错误的。

Who wants to be the official who formally recognises any of these things? What began as buying time increasingly becomes a bureaucratic reflex: do not recognise the loss, do not clear the market, do not admit the mistake.  
有哪个官员愿意成为正式承认其中任何一项后果的人？最初为了“争取时间”的做法，日益演变成一种官僚式的本能反应：不确认损失、不出清市场、不承认错误。

### The habit of hiding weakness through investment
### 用投资掩盖虚弱的习惯

China’s other great macroeconomic instinct is to respond to weakness by mobilising more capital and producing more.  
中国另一种强大的宏观经济本能，是通过动员更多资本和增加输出来应对虚弱。

For most of the reform era, that made sense. China was poor, capital-starved and underbuilt. It needed roads, ports, factories, housing, electricity and entire cities. Investment generated high returns, created employment and rapidly expanded productive capacity.  
在改革开放的大部分时期，这样做是合理的。当时的中国贫穷、缺乏资本且基础设施严重不足。它需要道路、港口、工厂、住房、电力以及整座整座的城市。投资带来了丰厚的回报，创造了就业，并迅速扩大了生产能力。

That model rested on a broader bargain. Household savings were channelled through banks into infrastructure, property, local governments, state companies and manufacturing. Cheap capital went to producers; household consumption took second place. Household welfare has always taken a back seat to state-directed accumulation in the CCP’s Marxist economic model. For a developing country, it was extraordinarily successful.  
该模式建立在一个更广泛的默契交易之上。居民储蓄通过银行体系被定向输送到基础设施、房地产、地方政府、国有企业和制造业中。廉价资本流向了生产者；居民消费被退居次席。在中国共产党的马克思主义经济模式中，家庭福利从来都排在国家主导的积累之后。对于一个发展中国家而言，这套做法曾取得了极其巨大的成功。

---

But China today does not suffer from a shortage of productive capacity. It increasingly suffers from insufficient household demand relative to that capacity. Yet the instinct remains the same. When growth slows, invest more. When a locality struggles, build another industrial zone. When a politically favoured technology emerges, provide credit, land, subsidies and state capital.  
但今天的中国并不面临生产能力不足的问题。相对于这种产能，它面临的日益严重的矛盾是居民需求的不足。然而，那种本能依然如故。当增长放缓时，加大投资。当某个地方经济挣扎时，再建一个工业园区。当某种在政治上受到青睐的技术出现时，给予信贷、土地、补贴和国有资本的支持。

Cheap capital reinforces the pattern by allowing politically favoured borrowers and projects to survive even as returns deteriorate. Local-government competition reinforces it again: every province wants semiconductors, every city wants electric vehicles, every industrial zone wants the next politically favoured industry. The question becomes less Does China need this capacity? and more If we do not build it, will another province get it?  
廉价资本进一步巩固了这种模式——即允许政治上受宠的借款人和项目即使在回报率恶化的情况下依然得以存活。地方政府之间的竞争再次强化了这一点：每个省份都想要半导体，每个城市都想要新能源汽车，每个工业园区都想要下一个受到政治青睐的产业。大家思考的问题不再是“中国真的需要这个产能吗？”，而变成了“如果我们不建，是不是就被别的省份抢去了？”。

---

When domestic demand cannot absorb the output, the global market becomes the escape valve. That worked when China was much smaller. It becomes far more difficult when China’s industrial system is so large that its excess production reshapes global markets and provokes resistance abroad. What once formed a virtuous circle—high savings, high investment, expanding production and exports—can turn into a cycle of weak consumption, overinvestment, falling returns and external friction.  
当国内需求无法消化产出时，全球市场就成了泄洪阀。在中国体量尚小时，这一套行之有效。但当中国的工业体系庞大到其过剩产能足以重塑全球市场并引发外部抵制时，这就变得困难得多了。曾经形成的良性循环——高储蓄、高投资、产能扩大与出口——如今可能转变为消费疲软、过度投资、回报率下降以及外部摩擦的恶性循环。

### The state–household imbalance
### 国家与家庭之间的失衡

This is not merely an investment problem. It reflects a deeper distributional structure.  
这不仅仅是一个投资问题。它反映出一种更深层次的分配结构。

For decades, the system channelled a large share of national resources toward the state, producers and capital formation while households carried much of the burden of housing, education, healthcare and retirement. During the high-growth era, rapid income gains and rising asset values made that bargain tolerable.  
几十年来，该体系将国家的大部分资源导向了国家机器、生产者和资本形成，而普通家庭则承担了住房、教育、医疗和养老的大部分沉重负担。在高增长时代，收入的快速增加和资产价值的上涨使这种默契尚可被忍受。

Today it increasingly suppresses demand. Households facing uncertainty about employment, property wealth, healthcare, education and retirement rationally save more. Yet the state continues to respond largely by trying to induce spending at the margin rather than fundamentally changing the distribution of income, wealth and risk.  
而今天，它正日益压制需求。面对就业、房产财富、医疗、教育和养老的不确定性，普通家庭理性地选择增加储蓄。然而，政府的应对方式在很大程度上依然只是试图在边际上刺激消费，而不是从根本上改变收入、财富和风险的分配格局。

A serious shift toward household-led demand would require more than occasional consumption subsidies. It would require shifting resources and security toward households. And that begins to collide with the existing structure of the Party-state.  
要真正转向由家庭需求驱动的模式，需要的绝不仅仅是偶尔发放的消费补贴。它需要将资源和安全保障向普通家庭倾斜。而这一点，便开始与党国体制的既有结构发生正面碰撞。

### Where reform runs into the Party-state
### 改革在何处撞上党国体制

This is the ultimate limit.  
这就是终极的极限。

China faces reforms that may be economically desirable but politically difficult because they would reduce the Party-state’s control over capital, information, institutions or society.  
中国面临着一些在经济上或许切中要害、但在政治上却难以为继的改革，因为这些改革会削弱党国体制对资本、信息、制度或社会的控制。

A more market-driven financial system would allocate capital more ruthlessly, but it would also reduce the state’s ability to direct cheap financing toward local governments, state companies and politically favoured sectors. More secure private property rights would encourage long-term investment, but genuinely secure property rights mean imposing limits on the government’s discretion.  
一个更加由市场主导的金融体系将会更加冷酷无情地配置资本，但它也会削弱国家将廉价资金导向地方政府、国有企业和政治受宠部门的能力。更受保障的私有财产权固然会鼓励长期投资，但真正受到保障的财产权，意味着要对政府的行政裁量权施加约束。

Greater local fiscal autonomy could improve incentives, but greater local autonomy also weakens central control. Freer circulation of information would improve decision-making, but it would reduce the Party’s control over information. More independent courts would make commercial rules more predictable, but genuinely independent courts would also constrain the Party. A larger household share of national income would strengthen consumption, but it would also mean fewer resources available to the state-led investment machine.  
赋予地方更大的财税自主权可以改善激励机制，但地方自主权的扩大同样会削弱中央控制。更自由的信息流通能够改善决策质量，但它会削弱党对信息的掌控。更独立的法院会让商业规则更具可预期性，但真正独立的司法体系也同样会约束党本身的权力。居民收入占国民收入比重的提高会拉动消费，但这也意味着国家主导的投资机器所能支配的资源变少了。

---

At some point, economic adaptation runs directly into political power. The issue is not that Beijing does not understand the problem. The issue is that some solutions would weaken the institutional interests of the Party-state. That creates a wall around reform.  
在某个节点上，经济层面的自我调适会直接撞上政治权力。问题不在于北京不理解这个问题。问题在于，某些解法会削弱党国体制本身的制度利益。这就给改革筑起了一道高墙。

China’s political system wants the efficiency produced by markets, private enterprise, open information and international integration while increasingly resisting the autonomy those mechanisms require. Xi’s reversals in Section I are partly a response to that contradiction. The forces unleashed by the reform era produced enormous wealth and dynamism, but they also created centres of influence and autonomy outside direct Party control.  
中国的政治体制既想要市场、民营企业、开放信息和国际融合所带来的效率，又日益抗拒这些机制运作所必需的自主权。第一部分中提到的习近平的倒退，部分正是对这一矛盾做出的回应。改革开放时代释放出的力量创造了巨大的财富和活力，但它们也在党的直接控制之外，创造出了一个个权力与自主性的中心。

Xi chose political control. The economic cost is increasingly visible.  
习近平选择了政治控制。而其经济代价正变得越来越清晰可见。

---

## III. The Bedrock Is Changing
## 三、 根基正在改变

The third problem cannot simply be blamed on Xi. China itself is changing.  
第三个问题不能简单地归咎于习近平。中国本身正在发生改变。

The population is declining, society is ageing and the working-age population is shrinking. The great movement of people from countryside to city that powered earlier decades has reached its limits. China has already built enormous amounts of infrastructure. Housing demand cannot expand indefinitely. Debt levels are far higher than they were twenty years ago.  
人口在减少，社会在老龄化，劳动年龄人口在萎缩。曾经驱动了前几十年发展的庞大城乡人口流动，已经触及极限。中国已经建造了极其庞大的基础设施。住房需求不可能无休止地扩张。债务水平远高于二十年前。

China has also moved much closer to the technological frontier, where the easy gains from importing, copying and improving existing technologies become harder to capture. These are not primarily political mistakes. They are consequences of development, earlier policy choices and long-term demographic change. Even a highly competent Chinese government would find them difficult to confront.  
中国也已经距离技术前沿近得多，在这一阶段，靠引进、抄袭和改进既有技术来轻松获利的红利变得越来越难以获取。这些本质上并不是政治上的失误。它们是发展本身、早期政策选择以及长期人口结构变化所带来的必然后果。即使是一个极其高效的中国政府，也会发现应对这些挑战困难重重。

---

The external environment has changed as well. For decades, China developed in a world unusually accommodating to its growth model. Foreign capital flowed in, technology was accessible, multinational companies built supply chains around China, Western markets absorbed rapidly growing Chinese exports, and universities, research institutions and businesses expanded exchanges.  
外部环境同样发生了改变。几十年来，中国是在一个对其增长模式异常包容的世界里发展的。外资源源不断涌入，技术唾手可得，跨国公司围绕中国构建供应链，西方市场消化着快速增长的中国出口产品，高校、科研机构和企业之间的交流不断扩大。

China was able to pursue an investment-heavy, export-oriented growth model partly because the outside world was willing to absorb its output and deepen its economic dependence on China. That environment is less forgiving today.  
中国之所以能够推行重投资、出口导向型的增长模式，部分是因为外部世界愿意吸收其产出，并加深对中国的经济依赖。而如今，那个环境不再那么宽容了。

---

Some of that change is the consequence of Xi’s own policies and therefore belongs in Section I. But some also reflects China’s sheer scale. An economy that once entered world markets as a relatively small producer has become large enough that its internal imbalances can reshape global industries. When Chinese excess production enters foreign markets at scale, other countries respond. When dependence on Chinese supply chains becomes economically or politically significant, governments try to reduce it.  
这其中的某些变化是习近平自身政策的结果，因而属于第一部分讨论的内容。但也有一部分反映了中国本身的庞大规模。一个曾经作为相对微小的生产者进入世界市场的经济体，如今已成长为庞然大物，其内部失衡足以重塑全球产业。当中国的过剩产能大规模涌入外国市场时，其他国家会做出回应。当对中国供应链的依赖在经济或政治上变得举足轻重时，各国政府就会试图去降低这种依赖。

The world’s capacity and willingness to absorb China’s domestic imbalance is therefore no longer what it once was. By themselves, these structural changes would already make the next stage of China’s development much harder. China now needs higher productivity from a shrinking workforce, more efficient use of a much larger debt stock, new sources of demand after the property boom and greater innovation as the easy gains from catch-up diminish. It also has less room to rely on external markets to absorb the consequences of domestic overinvestment.  
因此，世界吸收中国国内失衡的能力和意愿已大不如前。仅凭这些结构性变化本身，就已经会让中国下一阶段的发展变得艰难得多。中国如今需要从日益萎缩的劳动力中榨取更高的生产率，需要更加高效地利用庞大得多的债务存量，需要在房地产繁荣退场后寻找新的需求来源，并在追赶红利递减的同时实现更大程度的创新。它依靠外部市场来消化国内过度投资后果的空间也变小了。

The conditions are simply less forgiving.  
条件已经变得不再那么宽松。

---

## IV. How the Three Forces Deepen the Decay
## 四、 三种力量如何加剧衰变

This is where the three forces come together. They do not simply coexist. They reinforce one another.  
这正是三种力量汇聚交织的地方。它们并非仅仅并行存在。它们在相互强化。

The changing bedrock makes the old governing model less effective. Slower growth means losses can no longer be diluted as easily. Ageing makes productivity more important. Higher debt makes wasted capital more costly. Property saturation makes continued construction less useful. Technological maturity increases the value of experimentation and information. A less accommodating world makes export dependence more difficult.  
根基的变化使旧有的治理模式效能下降。增长放缓意味着损失再也无法像过去那样轻松被稀释。老龄化使生产率变得更加举足轻重。更高的债务使资本浪费的代价更加高昂。房地产的饱和使继续大兴土木的边际效应大打折扣。技术上的日益成熟，抬高了探索尝试与信息透明的价值。一个不再那么包容的世界，使出口依赖变得难以为继。

---

These changes demand a different kind of adaptation. China needs to recognise losses faster, allocate capital more ruthlessly, strengthen household demand, tolerate experimentation, encourage entrepreneurial risk-taking and receive more accurate information from below. Yet the inherited governing model pushes in the opposite direction. Losses are postponed, weakness is answered with more investment, capital continues to flow toward politically favoured borrowers and producers, and structural problems are managed rather than resolved.  
这些变化迫切需要一种完全不同的自我调适。中国需要更快地确认损失、更加冷酷无情地配置资本、拉动居民需求、包容试点探索、鼓励企业家承担风险，并从基层获取更加准确的信息。然而，沿袭下来的治理模式却在向相反的方向推挤。损失被推迟确认、虚弱被以更多的投资来回应、资本继续流向政治受宠的借款者和生产者、结构性问题只是被维持管控，而非得到根本解决。

---

The Party-state’s own interests then place limits on deeper reform. Greater market discipline reduces political control over capital. Stronger property rights constrain government discretion. More household income means fewer resources controlled by the state. More local autonomy weakens central command. More open information reduces the Party’s control over information. The reforms needed to adapt the economy therefore increasingly collide with the political structure itself.  
党国体制自身的利益随后对更深层次的改革设下了藩篱。更严格的市场纪律会削弱政治对资本的控制。更受保障的财产权会约束政府的行政裁量权。更多的居民收入意味着国家所掌控的资源变少。更多的地方自主权会削弱中央发号施令的效力。更开放的信息流动会降低党对信息的掌控。因此，调适经济所需的改革，日益与政治结构本身发生正面碰撞。

---

Xi’s reversal deepens the problem further. A country that needs more experimentation becomes more centralised. An economy that needs more entrepreneurial risk-taking becomes more politically intrusive. A mature system that needs honest information places tighter constraints on what can be said and reported. A government that needs to recognise failure makes admitting error increasingly dangerous.  
习近平的倒退进一步加剧了这一难题。一个需要更多探索试点的国家，变得更加集权。一个需要企业家承担风险的经济体，政治干预却越来越深。一个需要真实信息的成熟体系，对言论和报道的限制却越来越严。一个需要面对失败的政府，却让承认错误变得越来越危险。

---

This affects behaviour throughout society. Officials become more cautious, entrepreneurs shorten their investment horizons, households save more and commit less, banks avoid recognising losses and local governments keep weak projects alive. None of these actors has to believe that China is failing. They merely have to respond rationally to the incentives in front of them. That is enough.  
这影响着整个社会的行为模式。官员们变得更加谨小慎微、企业家们缩短了投资周期、家庭增加储蓄并减少消费承诺、银行极力避免确认损失、地方政府则强行维持着毫无效益的项目。这些行为主体中的任何一方都不需要认为“中国正在走向失败”。他们只需要对摆在眼前的激励机制做出理性反应就足够了。仅此一点，就已足够。

---

The deterioration in expectations then becomes a force of its own. A household that expects weaker income growth saves more. An entrepreneur who expects greater political uncertainty invests less. An official who expects that initiative creates personal risk does less. A bank that expects the government to prevent visible failure keeps extending credit. Each decision weakens the adjustment mechanism a little further.  
预期的恶化随后演变成了一种自发的破坏力量。预判收入增长放缓的家庭会存更多的钱。预判政治不确定性增加的企业家会减少投资。预判主动作为会带来个人风险的官员会选择少干少错。预判政府会阻止显性爆雷的银行会不断展期信贷。每一个这样的决策，都让自我调节机制进一步被削弱。

---

Weak household confidence suppresses consumption. Weak consumption encourages more state-directed investment. More investment produces more debt and excess capacity. More debt makes losses harder to recognise. The refusal to recognise losses traps capital in less productive uses. Slower growth then creates even greater pressure to postpone failure and mobilise more resources. The system becomes caught in a loop.  
微弱的家庭信心抑制了消费。疲软的消费倒逼出更多国家主导的投资。更多的投资催生出更多的债务和过剩产能。更多的债务使损失越来越难以被确认。拒绝确认损失将资本锁死在了低效的用途中。而增速放缓随后又创造出更大的压力，迫使系统去推迟失败并动员更多资源。整个体系陷入了一场怪圈死循环。

---

There is also an information problem. A country facing simple problems can sometimes govern effectively through command. A mature economy facing complex trade-offs requires feedback. It needs officials willing to report that a policy is failing, businesses willing to expose problems, local governments able to experiment and institutions capable of challenging assumptions.  
此外，还存在一个信息传递的问题。一个面对简单问题的国家，有时可以通过行政指令实现有效治理。而一个面临复杂权衡的成熟经济体，则需要反馈机制。它需要官员敢于上报某种政策正在走向失败、需要企业敢于揭露问题、需要地方政府能够进行试点探索、并需要有机构能够去质疑既有的前提假设。

Yet political centralisation creates incentives to filter information upward. Bad news carries risk, contradicting the prevailing line carries risk, and taking initiative carries risk. The centre therefore gains more formal control while potentially receiving less reliable information about the system it controls.  
然而，政治上的高度集权创造出了一种将信息向上过滤过滤再过滤的激励机制。坏消息带有风险、违背既定主基调带有风险、展现主动性同样带有风险。因此，中央虽然获得了更多名义上的控制权，但其所掌控体系反馈回来的信息，可靠性却可能大打折扣。

---

This is the deepest form of corrosion. It is not simply the accumulation of bad outcomes. It is the weakening of the mechanisms that would normally force those outcomes to be corrected.  
这是最深层次的腐蚀。它不仅仅是坏结果的堆积。而是那种通常能够倒逼这些坏结果得到纠正的机制本身正在衰退。

The weaker the underlying conditions become, the more the state relies on the tools it already knows: control, investment, credit, administrative intervention and delay. The more heavily it relies on those tools, the more it weakens the mechanisms China increasingly needs: market discipline, private initiative, honest information, stronger household confidence and willingness to accept failure.  
底层条件越是恶化，国家就越是依赖它所熟知的旧工具：控制、投资、信贷、行政干预以及拖延。而它对这些工具依赖得越深，就越是会削弱中国如今迫切需要的机制：市场纪律、民营领域的积极性、真实的信息、更强劲的居民信心以及接受失败的意愿。

---

## Conclusion
## 结语

China can continue building factories, producing technologies, expanding military power and mobilising resources on a scale few countries can match. But, outsiders should not mistake that visible strength for underlying health. A powerful state can remain powerful even as it becomes less adaptive, less efficient and less capable of correcting its own mistakes. The danger is to look at the branches and assume the roots must be healthy.  
中国可以继续以极少数国家所能企及的规模建造工厂、生产技术、扩张军力和动员资源。然而，外界绝不应将这种肉眼可见的强大误认为是底层的健康状态。一个强大的国家即使变得越来越缺乏适应能力、效率低下且越来越无力纠正自身错误，它依然可以在外表上保持强大。危险之处在于，只看到繁茂的枝干，就一厢情愿地以为根基必然健康。

---

There is a saying I increasingly hear: Xi Jinping may become the last emperor of the Chinese Communist Party. I do not know whether that will prove true. But the irony is clear.  
我听到一句越来越多被提及的话：习近平可能会成为中国共产党的最后一任皇帝。我不知道这句话最终是否会应验。但其中的讽刺意味是显而易见的。

Xi has concentrated power to protect the Party, weakened institutions to strengthen control, and closed political and economic space to make the system more secure. In doing so, he may be destroying the very capacity for adaptation that allowed the CCP to survive its earlier mistakes.  
习近平集中权力是为了保护党，削弱制度是为了加强控制，收紧政治与经济空间是为了让体制更加安全。然而在此过程中，他可能恰恰正在摧毁曾经让中国共产党得以在早期的历史错误中幸存下来的自我调适能力。

---

The greatest threat to the CCP may therefore not come from the forces Xi has spent his rule trying to suppress. It may come from what he has done to the system itself.  
因此，对中国共产党最大的威胁，或许并非来自习近平在其统治期间极力镇压的那些力量。它可能恰恰来自他对自己这个体制所做的一切。

And the greatest mistake outsiders can make is to confuse the power that system can still project with the health of the system producing it.  
而外界所能犯的最大错误，就是将该体制依然能够投射出的强大力量，与产生这种力量的体制本身的健康状况混为一谈。
