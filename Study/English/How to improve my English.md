然而，这肯定不是中国领导人所想的。

They have their own internal sources of information; the public, in their view, need not be concerned by affairs of the state.  
他们有自己的内部信息来源；在他们看来，公众不必关心国家事务。

This is what they have been building towards.  
这正是他们一直在朝着建设的方向。

For the party, the power to set the agenda in the modern age—to control so much of what people see and say—is a triumph.  
对党来说，在现代设定议程的权力——控制人们所见所闻的如此之多——是一种胜利。
