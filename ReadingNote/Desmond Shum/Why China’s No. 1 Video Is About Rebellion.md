# Why China’s No. 1 Video Is About Rebellion
# 为什么中国最火的视频是关于“造反”的？

**Desmond Shum**  
**沈栋**

**Aug 21, 2026**  
**2026年8月21日**

---

These days I spend more time trying to gauge social sentiment in China.  
这些天，我花更多的时间试图去推测和感知中国的社会情绪。

Partly because I no longer live there and no longer feel it through daily life.  
一部分原因是我不再生活在那里，无法通过日常生活去亲身感受它。

But also because sentiment is shifting in ways we rarely saw when the economy seemed to move only one way: up.  
但也是因为这种情绪正在发生转变，而在过去经济似乎只朝一个方向——即“向上”移动的年代里，我们很少能见到这样的转变。

Over the past few days, a 26-minute AI-generated historical short film has gone viral in China.  
在过去几天里，一部26分钟的AI生成历史短片在中国爆红网络。

《妖异簿·问苍生》 (The Book of Strange Phenomena: Ask the People), uploaded to Bilibili around mid-August by a relatively small creator, has received more than 7 million views and is now No. 1 on Bilibili’s overall hot list.  
《妖异簿·问苍生》由一位粉丝量并不大的创作者于8月中旬左右上传至哔哩哔哩（B站），目前已获得超过700万次的播放量，并荣登B站全站热门榜第一名。

It is classic 借古喻今 — using the past to speak about the present.  
这是经典的借古喻今——用过去的事情来隐喻当下的现实。

The story is set in the dying years of the Eastern Han.  
故事设定在东汉末年。

Its narrator is an imperial official whose job is to investigate reports of 妖 — demons and supernatural phenomena.  
故事的叙述者是一位朝廷官员，他的职责是调查有关“妖”——即妖怪和超自然现象的汇报。

Again and again, he discovers that the “demons” are really ordinary people driven by poverty, abuse and desperation.  
他一次又一次地发现，所谓的“妖怪”，其实都是被贫困、虐待和绝望逼上绝路的普通百姓。

A beggar invents a deity because poor people cannot afford doctors.  
乞丐捏造神明，是因为穷人根本看不起郎中。

A son carves his dead father’s face into a tree because officials beat the father to death and disposed of his body.  
儿子把死去的父亲的容貌雕刻在树上，是因为官吏将父亲活活打死并抛尸荒野。

Others manufacture supernatural events because ordinary grievances cannot reach the emperor.  
还有人伪造超自然事件，是因为普通人的冤屈根本无法传达给皇帝。

Sometimes the narrator knowingly records these human acts as “demons,” because admitting the truth would expose official wrongdoing — and get those involved killed.  
有时，叙述者明知这是人为所为，却依然记录为“妖”，因为承认真相就会揭露官吏的罪行——并让相关的人招致杀身之祸。

Eventually he encounters Zhang Jue and the Yellow Turbans.  
最终，他遇到了张角和黄巾军。

What the court calls sorcery and rebellion, he comes to see as the product of a society breaking down: people without food, medicine, justice or any effective way to be heard.  
朝廷眼中的“妖术”与“谋反”，在他看来不过是一个正在走向崩溃的社会的必然产物：人们没有粮食、没有医药、没有正义，也没有任何可以有效发声的途径。

Zhang Jue’s real “magic” is giving people who had resigned themselves to suffering a reason to stop accepting their fate.  
张角真正的“法术”，是给那些本已对苦难认命的人们一个不再接受命运安排的理由。

The parallels with today’s China are hard to miss.  
这与当今中国的相似之处令人无法忽视。

Again and again, officials are less interested to correct what’s wrong than in how to classify it so responsibility does not reach the state.  
官员们一次又一次地对纠正错误毫无兴趣，反而更关心如何对其进行分类与定性，从而让责任不至于延伸到国家身上。

If unrest is caused by people, then one may ask whether it is 官逼民反 — “official oppression driving the people to revolt,” or whether the court has failed, or even whether the emperor has lost virtue.  
如果是人为造成的动荡，那么人们就可能会追问这是否是“官逼民反”，或是朝廷失职，甚至皇帝是否失德。

Far safer to call it 妖 — demonic influence and blame those who have “bewitched” the population.  
而将其称之为“妖”——归咎于妖魔作祟或那些“蛊惑”民众的力量，显然要安全得多。

Today the vocabulary maybe different but share the same context: “境外敌对势力” — “foreign hostile forces,” “别有用心的人” — “people with ulterior motives,” “被煽动的群众” — “masses who have been incited.”  
今天使用的词汇或许有所不同，但其语境如出一辙：“境外敌对势力”、“别有用心的人”、“被煽动的群众”。

The political function is similar: turn a grievance about governance into a problem of manipulation, subversion or security.  
其政治功能也是相似的：将对社会治理的不满，转化为操纵、颠覆或国家安全的问题。

The film also shows officials protecting the face of the court rather than confronting reality.  
这部短片还展示了官员们宁愿维护朝廷的面子，也不愿面对现实。

Bad news is rewritten, responsibility pushed elsewhere, and suffering disappears behind an official label.  
坏消息被重写，责任被推给别处，而苦难则消失在官方给出的标签背后。

That too feels familiar.  
这听起来同样令人感到熟悉。

When ordinary speech does not work, people turn to jokes, coded language and historical allegory.  
当正常的言语不再管用时，人们就会转向段子、隐喻和历史影射。

In the film, “demons” speak because ordinary people cannot.  
在影片中，“妖”之所以发声，是因为普通人无法发声。

Today Chinese still casually call the government 官府 — “the authorities” or 朝廷 — “the imperial court.”  
如今，中国人仍然习惯性地将政府戏称为“官府”或“朝廷”。

They increasingly call themselves 牛马 — “beasts of burden” and 韭菜 — “chives,” repeatedly harvested by others.  
他们越来越频繁地称自己为“牛马”和被一茬茬割掉的“韭菜”。

These are self deprecations. But they are revealing.  
这些都是自嘲，但它们却十分耐人寻味。

For decades, rapid growth papers over many fundamental schisms.  
几十年来，经济的高速增长掩盖了许多根本性的分歧与割裂。

The pie kept expanding.  
蛋糕一直在做大。

Officials, SOEs, private sector and ordinary households could all get more, even if their shares were wildly unequal.  
官员、国企、民营企业和普通家庭都能分得更多，即使各自所占的份额极其不平等。

As long as most people believed tomorrow would be better than today, resentment could be absorbed by growth.  
只要大多数人相信明天会比今天更好，怨恨与不满就能被增长所消化。

Covid and the economic deterioration that followed changed that psychology.  
疫情以及随之而来的经济恶化改变了这种心理。

Property values fell.  
资产（房价）下跌。

Businesses struggled.  
企业艰难挣扎。

Employment weakened.  
就业市场走弱。

Expectations came down.  
社会预期普遍降低。

And once the era of easy incremental growth ends, distribution becomes much more zero-sum.  
而一旦轻松实现增量增长的时代结束，利益分配就会变得非常趋向零和博弈。

The question is no longer simply who gets more, but increasingly who has to give something up so someone else can preserve his share.  
问题不再仅仅是谁能分得更多，而日益变成了谁必须做出牺牲，才能让其他人保住既得的份额。

In Chinese, 钱不够分了 — there is no longer enough money to go around.  
用中文来说，就是“钱不够分了”。

That gives today’s language of beasts of burden and chives a darker meaning.  
这给今天关于“牛马”和“韭菜”的表达赋予了更加阴暗的含义。

People increasingly feel they are not simply working hard, but being extracted from to keep a system functioning.  
人们越来越强烈地感觉到，自己不仅仅是在努力工作，而是在被源源不断地抽取资源，以维持某个体制的运转。

There is even a scene in the film where starving people discover official granaries piled high with grain.  
影片中甚至有这样一个场景：饥饿的百姓发现官府的粮仓里堆满了粮食。

The question is not whether resources exist.  
问题不在于资源是否存在。

It is who controls them, who gets them, and whom the system ultimately serves.  
而在于谁掌控着资源、谁获取了资源，以及这个体制究竟最终在为谁服务。

When millions experience similar problems simultaneously, it becomes harder to explain them as millions of individual failures.  
当数以百万计的人同时面临类似的困境时，就很难再将其解释为千百万个个体的失败了。

People begin asking whether the problem is the system.  
人们开始追问：问题是不是出在体制本身？

And in China that question inevitably leads toward the CCP, because the Party claims sole authority over that system.  
而在中国，这个问题不可避免地会指向中国共产党，因为该党声称对这一体制拥有绝对的领导权。

As Xi Jinping put it: “东西南北中，党领导一切” — “East, west, south, north and center: the Party leads everything.”  
正如习近平所说：“党政军民学，东西南北中，党是领导一切的。”

When things go well, the Party takes the credit.  
当一切顺遂时，功劳归于党。

When things go badly for long enough, people naturally start assigning responsibility in the same direction.  
当情况持续恶化足够长的时间后，人们自然也会顺理成章地将责任归咎于同一个方向。

Another line in the film captures this: “What people say doesn’t count. Only when demons speak does the court become afraid.”  
影片中的另一句台词点破了这一点：“百姓说话是不算数的，只有妖说话，朝廷才会害怕。”

Its portrayal of Zhang Jue is therefore striking.  
因此，影片对张角的刻画令人印象深刻。

Instead of asking simply how a cult leader deceived so many peasants, it asks a more important question: Why were so many people ready to follow him?  
它并没有简单地追问一个邪教首领是如何欺骗了这么多农民，而是提出了一个更为关键的问题：为什么会有这么多人愿意跟随他？

The narrator concludes: “Zhang Jue did not create this upheaval. He simply heard the rumbling beneath the ground before the imperial court did.”  
叙述者总结道：“张角并未制造这场动乱，他不过是比朝廷更早听到了地下的轰鸣声。”

Zhang Jue tells his followers: “You were not born to kneel. You were not born to starve.”  
张角对他的追随者说：“你们生来不是为了跪着的，你们生来不是为了挨饿的。”

At that point, the film is describing a change in consciousness: people moving from asking for relief from suffering to asking why they should have to live this way at all.  
在那个节点上，影片描述的是一种思想觉悟的转变：人们从“请求减轻苦难”，转向追问“自己究竟为什么非要这样活着”。

It ends: “When later generations read of demons, do not ask about ghosts and gods. Ask about the people.”  
影片结尾写道：“后人读《妖异簿》，莫问鬼神，问苍生。”

I don’t believe China today is ripe for rebellion. We are not there yet.  
我不认为当今中国具备了造反的成熟条件，我们还没有走到那一步。

But the direction of travel matters.  
但发展的走向至关重要。

More Chinese appear to be moving from: “What did I do wrong?” to: “What is wrong with the system?”  
似乎有越来越多的中国人正在完成这种转变：从“我做错了什么？”，转向“体制出了什么问题？”

That shift in consciousness is more consequential than any isolated protest.  
这种认知上的转变，比任何孤立的抗议活动都更具深远影响。

For decades, economic growth prevented many Chinese from asking too hard who the system really served.  
几十年来，经济增长让许多中国人未能深入追问这个体制究竟在为谁服务。

Economic decline is forcing that question into view.  
而经济的衰退正迫使这个问题浮出水面。

For a regime whose legitimacy has rested heavily on rising living standards and competent rule, that trend should be alarming.  
对于一个其合法性严重依赖于生活水平提高和高效治理的政权来说，这种趋势应当是令人警惕的。

Perhaps that is why a 26-minute film about demons, peasants and a rebellion nearly 1,850 years ago is today the No. 1 video on Bilibili.  
或许，这就是为什么一部关于近1850年前的妖怪、农民与反抗的26分钟短片，能在今天登顶B站热门榜榜首的原因。
