# A Stronger Xi, a Weaker System

# 更强大的习近平，更脆弱的体制

## What Happens When Nobody Knows the Rules Anymore?

## 当所有人都不再懂得规则，会发生什么？

**Desmond Shum**

**沈栋**

**Sep 01, 2026**

**2026年9月1日**

---

Two of the most revealing political personnel developments in China today are the fall of Zhong Shaojun and the unusual rise of a group of so-called “san-fei” officials.

当今中国最能透露政治信号的两个人事动态，莫过于钟绍军的落马以及一群所谓“三非”官员的异常崛起。

At first glance, they may appear unrelated.

乍一看，这两件事似乎毫无关联。

Put them alongside what has happened to Wang Qishan and his network, however, and I think they reveal something larger about Xi Jinping’s system of rule.

然而，如果将它们与王岐山及其网络所遭遇的一切放在一起，我认为它们揭示了关于习近平统治体制更深层次的某种东西。

I have written several times about Wang Qishan, both in my book and in my posts.

关于王岐山，无论是在我的书中还是在我的发文中，我都曾多次写过。

Wang was indispensable to Xi during his first term.

在习近平的第一任期内，王岐山是不可或缺的。

As head of the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection, he led the anti-corruption campaign that tore through the Party-state bureaucracy.

作为中央纪律检查委员会（中纪委）的掌门人，他领导了席卷整个党政官僚体系的反腐败运动。

Whatever one thinks of the campaign’s motives, its political effect was clear: old patronage networks were destroyed, senior officials who had previously seemed untouchable were taken down, and Xi dramatically strengthened his control over the bureaucracy.

无论人们如何看待这场运动的动机，其政治效果是显而易见的：旧有的依附庇护网络被摧毁，此前看似不可撼动的高级官员被拿下，习近平极大地加强了对官僚体系的控制。

Zhong Shaojun performed a remarkably similar function in the military.

钟绍军在军队中扮演了极其相似的角色。

Zhong was a classmate of Whitney in her Tsinghua PhD class, a special program set up for promising senior CCP officials under Chen Xi.

钟绍军是 Whitney 在清华大学博士班的同学，那是陈希主导下专门为有前途的高级干部设立的特殊项目。

This group was groomed for national leadership.

这群人是被作为国家领导层培养的。

Everyone in that class understood Zhong to be one of Xi’s most trusted lieutenants.

那个班里的每一个人都知道，钟绍军是习近平最信任的得力干将之一。

He followed Xi from Zhejiang to Shanghai and then Beijing.

他一路追随习近平，从浙江到上海，再到北京。

After Xi took control of the Central Military Commission, Zhong entered the heart of the military leadership apparatus and became an important part of Xi’s effort to establish control over the PLA and dismantle the old military order associated with Guo Boxiong and Xu Caihou.

在习近平掌控中央军委之后，钟绍军进入了军事领导核心机构，成为习近平建立对解放军控制权、拆解与郭伯雄和徐才厚相关的旧军队秩序的重要组成部分。

So I see a striking parallel.

因此，我看到了一个惊人的平行现象。

Wang helped Xi clean out the civilian bureaucracy.

王岐山帮助习近平清理了文官官僚体系。

Zhong helped Xi clean out the military.

钟绍军帮助习近平清理了军队。

Wang himself retired without being formally purged.

王岐山本人退休了，未被正式清洗。

But much of the network surrounding him was subsequently dismantled.

但他身边的绝大部分网络随后都被拆解了。

A succession of people closely associated with him were investigated, imprisoned or politically removed.

一连串与他密切相关的人相继接受调查、入狱或在政治上被拔除。

Several eventually received suspended death sentences.

其中几人最终被判处死缓。

Now Zhong — someone who followed Xi for more than twenty years and helped him reconstruct control over the military — has himself fallen.

而如今钟绍军——一个追随了习近平二十多年并协助他重建对军队控制权的人——自己也倒下了。

The people who helped Xi dismantle the old power networks have discovered that even extraordinary service to Xi creates no permanent claim on his protection.

那些曾帮助习近平拆解旧权力网络的人发现，即便对习近平立下过汗马功劳，也无法换来对其庇护的永久索求权。

At the same time, something unusual is happening at the other end of the personnel system.

与此同时，在人事体系的另一端，某种不同寻常的事情也在发生。

A growing group of so-called “san-fei” officials are being elevated into major ministerial and provincial positions.

越来越多所谓的“三非”官员正被拔擢至关键的省部级岗位。

“San-fei” literally means “three-nots”:

“三非”字面意思就是“三个不是”：

not members of the Central Committee;

不是中央委员会委员；

not alternate members of the Central Committee;

不是中央委员会候补委员；

and not members of the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection.

并且不是中央纪律检查委员会委员。

These are not legal prerequisites for promotion.

这些并非晋升的法定前提条件。

But they are much more than useful indicators of who might advance next.

但它们绝不仅仅是预测谁可能下一步提拔的有用指标。

They are formal political statuses conferred by the Party Congress.

它们是由党代会赋予的正式政治身份。

They place an official inside the CCP’s nationally recognized elite hierarchy.

它们将一名官员置于中共全国认可的精英阶层序列之中。

A Central Committee member is not merely someone expected to receive an important job.

一位中央委员，不仅意味着他预料会获得重用。

He already possesses formal standing within the Party’s highest governing structure.

他已经在党内的最高治理结构中拥有了法定地位。

An alternate member has likewise been formally admitted into the national leadership cohort, albeit at a lower level.

候补委员同样被正式吸纳进了国家领导层梯队，尽管层级略低。

That is why the current rise of san-fei officials matters.

这正是当前“三非”官员崛起之所以重要的原因。

The leadership is not merely promoting people whom outsiders failed to predict.

最高领导层不仅仅是在提拔局外人未能预测到的人。

It is increasingly willing to bypass a political hierarchy that the Party itself formally established.

它正日益显露出绕过党自身所正式确立的政治等级秩序的意愿。

In the first half of 2026, five of nine newly filled provincial- or ministerial-level Party-government positions went to san-fei officials.

在2026年上半年，九个新填补的省部级党政职位中，有五个由“三非”官员接任。

Liu Xiaotao became governor of Jiangsu.

刘小涛出任江苏省省长。

Zhu Zhongming, also san-fei, has now been placed in charge of the Shanghai municipal government and is positioned to become mayor.

同为“三非”的朱忠明，现已被任命主持上海市政府工作，并拟任市长。

Kong Shaoxun has just moved from the CCP General Office to head the Gansu provincial government.

孔绍逊刚从中央办公厅调任甘肃省政府主要负责人。

Individual exceptions have always existed.

个别例外向来存在。

But repeated exceptions begin to change the meaning of the hierarchy itself.

但屡次出现的例外，开始改变等级制度本身的含义。

Formal political status within the Party is becoming less constraining on subsequent personnel decisions.

党内的正式政治身份，对后续人事决策的约束力正在减弱。

That is one message.

这是其中一层信息。

Zhong sends another.

钟绍军传递了另一层信息。

Long-term personal loyalty and extraordinary past contributions do not necessarily provide lasting political security.

长期的个人忠诚与过往的卓越贡献，并不一定能提供持久的政治安全。

And the dismantling of much of Wang Qishan’s network reinforces the same point.

而王岐山大部分网络的瓦解，进一步印证了这一点。

This matters particularly in China because the bureaucracy does not function through formal rules alone.

这一点在中国尤为重要，因为官僚体系的运行并不单靠正式规则。

It relies heavily on unwritten rules and on guanxi.

它高度依赖于成规潜规则以及“关系”。

Guanxi is often translated simply as “connections,” but that misses much of its function.

“关系”（Guanxi）常被简单翻译为“connections”（人脉），但这忽视了它的大部分功能。

In a political system where many of the most consequential rules are unwritten, guanxi helps people predict how other people will behave.

在一个许多最关键规则都未明文写出的政治体制中，关系有助于人们预判他人的行为模式。

Who introduced you?

谁介绍你入门的？

Who will vouch for you?

谁会为你担保？

Who keeps his word?

谁守信用？

Who remembers old allies?

谁还记得昔日盟友？

Who protects people who have served him?

谁会庇护那些为其效力过的人？

Who abandons people once they cease to be useful?

谁又会在他人失去利用价值后过河拆桥？

These judgments are part of the informal operating system of Chinese bureaucratic life.

这些判断，构成了中国官僚生活非正式操作系统的一部分。

That is why personal reputation matters so much.

正因如此，个人声誉才显得如此至关重要。

Officials spend entire careers building a political brand: whether they are dependable, competent, loyal, generous to others, vindictive, trustworthy or ruthless.

官员们用尽整个职业生涯去塑造自己的政治形象：究竟是可靠、干练、忠诚、宽厚，还是刻薄、值得信任，亦或是狠辣。

There is a Chinese judgment of character that is particularly damning:

中国传统文化中有一个评价人的词汇尤为致命：

boqing guayi — 薄情寡义.

薄情寡义（boqing guayi）。

It describes someone who places little value on old relationships or past obligations.

它形容一个人轻视旧日交情或过往恩义。

More importantly, it tells others that loyalty shown to this person should not be assumed to produce loyalty in return.

更重要的是，它在警告他人：对此人表现出的忠诚，绝不能指望换来等价的回报。

In Chinese culture, that is a serious judgment.

在中国文化中，这是一个极其沉重的定性。

In a guanxi-based environment, it is not merely a moral criticism.

在基于“关系”的环境里，这不单是道德上的批评。

It is a warning about counterparty risk.

它是一个关于“交易对方风险”（counterparty risk）的警示。

For an ordinary official, acquiring such a reputation can close career pathways.

对于普通官员而言，落得这样的名声可能会断送其仕途。

People become less willing to attach themselves to someone whose protection and reciprocity they cannot trust.

人们会变得不再愿意附庸于一个其庇护与回报不可靠的人。

But what happens when the person acquiring such a reputation sits at the apex of the entire system?

但是，如果落得这种名声的人，恰恰处于整个体制的最顶端，会发生什么？

That is where the current personnel developments become more consequential.

这正是当前人事变动更深远的影响所在。

The CCP has historically relied on two overlapping systems to create predictability.

历史上，中共依靠两个重叠的体系来建立可预测性。

One is organizational convention:

一是组织惯例：

career sequencing, seniority, Party Congress status, retirement norms and recognizable promotion paths.

履历序列、资历、党代会政治身份、退休规范以及可循的升迁路径。

The other is personal reciprocity:

二是个人互惠：

guanxi, patronage, loyalty, sponsorship and long-term relationships.

关系、派系庇护、忠诚、提携以及长期羁绊。

Neither is fully formal.

两者都非完全制度化。

But together they make the bureaucracy workable.

但它们共同维持了官僚体系的正常运转。

What happens when both become less reliable?

当这两者都变得不再可靠时，会发生什么？

The rise of san-fei officials suggests that formal political status and established personnel conventions can increasingly be overridden.

“三非”官员的崛起表明，正式的政治身份与既有的人事惯例可以越来越频繁地被打破。

Zhong’s fall suggests that even extraordinary personal loyalty creates no permanent protection.

钟绍军的落马表明，即便极其特殊的个人忠诚，也无法构成永久的护身符。

The Wang Qishan experience raises a related question: how much political value does past service retain once the function for which that service was needed has been completed?

而王岐山的经历则引出了一个相关问题：一旦某种服务所对应的功能已经完成，过往的付出还能保留多少政治价值？

There may be specific explanations for every individual case.

每一个具体案例或许都有其特殊解释。

But organizations do not respond only to individual explanations.

但组织机器不会仅仅对单个解释做出反应。

They respond to the pattern people believe they are observing.

它们会对人们认为自己观察到的模式做出反应。

And the emerging pattern is increasingly difficult to read.

而这个正在显现的模式，正变得越来越难以解读。

The old conventions appear less binding, yet no equally clear new set of conventions has emerged to replace them.

旧有的惯例似乎不再具备约束力，但与此同时，并未出现一套同样清晰的新惯例来取而代之。

That increases discretion at the top while reducing predictability below.

这极大地增加了顶层的自由裁量权，却大幅降低了底层的可预测性。

This matters because formal rules and guanxi have historically complemented one another inside the CCP system.

这之所以至关重要，是因为在中共体制内部，正式规则与“关系”历史上一直是互补的。

Where formal rules were incomplete, relationships, reputation and reciprocity helped fill the gap.

在正式规则存在空白的地方，人际关系、声誉与互惠机制填补了缝隙。

If both become less reliable, the problem is no longer simply uncertainty over who gets promoted.

如果两者都变得不可靠，问题就不再仅仅是“谁会升官”的不确定性。

It becomes a problem of organizational trust.

它演变成了组织信任问题。

Xi has spent much of his rule weakening independent political networks and concentrating authority in the centre.

习近平在其统治的大部分时间里，都在削弱独立的政治网络，并将权力高度集中于中央。

There is an obvious political logic to that.

这在政治逻辑上有其必然性。

But a large bureaucracy cannot function through command alone.

但庞大的官僚机器不可能仅凭命令运作。

People need some confidence that they understand how the organization works.

人们需要某种确信，相信自己理解这个组织是如何工作的。

The CCP has spent the Xi era asking whether officials are sufficiently loyal to the centre.

在习近平时代，中共一直在不断质问：官员们对中央是否足够忠诚？

But there is another side to that relationship:

但这种关系还有另一面：

Do officials believe the centre will reciprocate their loyalty?

官员们是否相信，中央会回报他们的忠诚？

That is not primarily a moral question.

这主要不是一个道德问题。

It is an organizational one.

而是一个组织学问题。

Loyalty without reciprocity can produce obedience.

没有互惠的忠诚或许能换来服从。

It does not necessarily produce trust.

它却不一定能换来信任。

And that may be the common thread connecting Wang Qishan’s network, Zhong Shaojun’s fall and the rise of the san-fei officials:

而这，或许正是连接王岐山网络瓦解、钟绍军落马以及“三非”官员崛起的那条共同主线：

the weakening of an old personnel order without a clearly observable replacement.

旧有人事秩序正在衰落，而清晰可见的新秩序尚未建立。

Xi may be gaining greater discretion over the bureaucracy.

习近平或许获得了对官僚体系更大的掌控与自由裁量权。

But discretion and institutional strength are not the same thing.

但自由裁量权并不等同于制度实力。

The paradox may be a stronger Xi sitting atop a weaker governing system.

这发生的悖论或许是：一个更强大的习近平，正坐在一台更加脆弱的国家治理机器之上。
