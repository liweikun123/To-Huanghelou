### 第八部分：庞氏骗局 HypeDrop 与主播 Juicy 的说辞

* **Clash.io's founder openly admitted on Twitter that less than 1% of Clash accounts make up 80% of their revenue.**
Clash.io的创始人曾在Twitter上公开承认：不到1%的账户贡献了他们80%的营收。
* **It's an open secret that these casinos are run on addicts.**
这些赌场靠重度成瘾者维系，早已是公开的秘密。
* **Now, this next part is pretty dark. We're going to talk about a specific casino owned by the same group as CSGORoll: HypeDrop.**
接下来的部分相当黑暗。我们要聊聊与CSGORoll属于同一母公司的另一家赌场：HypeDrop。
* **HypeDrop was a casino where, instead of gambling Counter-Strike skins, you could gamble real-world items like loot boxes for iPhones, Lamborghinis, or $10 gift cards.**
在HypeDrop上，玩家赌的不是游戏皮肤，而是实体物品战利品箱——里面包含从10美元礼品卡到兰博基尼跑车不等。
* **The real secret sauce of this website was a loyalty program called "The Daily Cases", where the more you spent, the higher level you got, and the more free money you'd get every day.**
该网站真正的杀手锏是名为“每日宝箱”的忠诚度计划：你充钱越多等级越高，每天就能领取越多的“免费资金”。
* **If that sounds unsustainable, it is. It was all part of a scheme to make the Danish government believe that HypeDrop was not actually gambling.**
这听起来根本不可持续，事实也确实如此。这完全是一场精心设计的阴谋，旨在让丹麦监管机构相信HypeDrop“不属于赌博”。
* **They argued players could not lose money on their site because over time, the daily cases paid back all the losses.**
他们辩称玩家在网站上绝不可能亏钱，因为长远来看，每日宝箱会填补所有亏损。
* **This was an unsustainable Ponzi scheme. On March 27th, 2024, Killian, the owner, announced he was shutting down the site, effectively rug-pulling his customers.**
这完全是个不可持续的庞氏骗局。2024年3月27日，老板Killian宣布关闭网站，直接对所有寄希望于靠每日宝箱回本的客户进行了“拔地毯（Rug Pull）”式的卷款跑路。
* **By removing their daily cases, HypeDrop stole from their users and revealed their legal loophole to be a lie.**
通过取消每日宝箱，HypeDrop洗劫了用户，并暴露出其所谓的法律漏洞纯属谎言。

### 第十部分：总结与罪魁祸首 Valve

* **We cannot fix this at the individual level of influencers, Esports teams, or even casinos. Doxxing or harassing people doesn't fix the systemic problem of incentives.**
我们无法在网红、电竞战队甚至赌场的个人层面上解决这个问题。对个人进行开盒或骚扰，无法改变背后的系统性利益驱动。
* **If we're serious about trying to solve this problem, we have to look farther up the food chain to the root cause: Valve.**
如果我们真想解决这个问题，就必须沿着食物链向上追溯到罪魁祸首：V社（Valve）。
* **Valve definitely has an underage gambling problem, and they really don't have an incentive to stop it.**
Valve绝对难逃干系，但他们根本没有动力去阻止未成年人赌博。
* **Underage, middle-age, overage—Valve is the kingpin reaping in all or the majority of the money.**
无论未成年还是成年，Valve才是赚取绝大部分利润的幕后最大赢家。
* **There's a reason why they haven't killed it yet. They have possessed the power for all these years, but they allow it to go and do the bare minimum crackdown just to keep it operating.**
这就是他们至今不斩草除根的原因。多年来他们一直拥有取缔它的权力，却选择放任自流，仅做最表面化的打压以维持其继续运转。
